Matthew Dumesny and Lachlan McHugh Win Adelaide NAPA Sprintcar Invitational Preliminary Features

Night 1 of the NAPA Sprintcar Invitational at the temporary NAPA Speedway inside the Adelaide Street Circuit produced two Preliminary A Main winners on Thursday, Nov. 26. American Joel Myers Jr. was second in the first and Hunter Schuerenberg third in the second.

Published November 27, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Matthew Dumesny and Lachlan McHugh won the two 20-lap Preliminary A Mains on Thursday, Nov. 26, the opening night of the NAPA Sprintcar Invitational at NAPA Speedway in Adelaide, South Australia, according to Speedcafe and the event’s Race Monitor timing and scoring. Dumesny (N57) beat Californian Joel Myers Jr. (USA46) by 0.852 seconds in the first feature. Jock Goodyer (T22) was third, James McFadden (WX23) fourth and Ryan Jones (S63) fifth. McHugh (NQ7) won the second by 0.064 seconds over Marcus Dumesny (N47), with American Hunter Schuerenberg (V88) third, Dylan Menz (Q46) fourth and Matt Egel (S29) fifth.

Dumesny took the lead from polesitter McFadden and led the rest of the way in a feature stopped several times, Speedcafe reported. In the last stoppage, Jones ran into the back of McFadden as the leaders slowed for a yellow, spinning both, but both continued. Dumesny said he thought he was running out of fuel with six laps to go and dedicated the win to his friend Grant Anderson, a fellow racer who died recently. Brendan Quinn was sixth and American Garet Williamson seventh, ahead of Tim Van Ginneken, Chris Harrison and Peter Doukas. New Zealander Max Guilford stopped with a mechanical problem.

In the second feature, Marcus Dumesny led almost half the race under green before lapped traffic bunched the leaders with 12 laps to go, according to Speedcafe. McHugh passed Menz for second with four laps left, caught Dumesny in traffic and took the lead with two laps remaining, and the two crossed the line almost side by side. Brock Hallett finished sixth, followed by Parker Scott, Ayrton Hodson, Michael Saller and Steven Loader, according to Race Monitor. The event, part of the Supercars bp Adelaide Grand Final weekend, ends with Friday night’s program in Adelaide, which falls in the early-morning hours of Friday, Nov. 27, U.S. Central time. Night 1 came five days after McHugh finished third and Matthew Dumesny crashed out of the Australian Sprintcar Championship at Toowoomba.

Sources: Dumesny takes opening night win despite fuel scare, Jack Fletcher, Speedcafe (Nov. 27, 2026); Race Monitor timing and scoring, NAPA Speedway in the City, Preliminary A Main 1 and Preliminary A Main 2 (marked unofficial by the timing provider; finishing order matches Speedcafe).
