Australian Sprintcar Championship Heads to Toowoomba Nov. 20-21

The 64th Nutrend Homes Australian Sprintcar Championship runs Nov. 20-21 at Hi-Tec Oils Toowoomba Speedway, the first time in 50 years the national title has been held there.

Published November 12, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The 64th Nutrend Homes Australian Sprintcar Championship is scheduled for Friday and Saturday, Nov. 20-21, at Hi-Tec Oils Toowoomba Speedway in Charlton, Queensland. The track’s official ticketing information says the national title is coming to Toowoomba for the first time in 50 years. Tasmanian Jock Goodyer is the reigning Australian No. 1.

A prelude meeting is set for Wednesday, Nov. 18, with sprint cars and speedcars. Championship Night 1 on Friday also includes V8 Dirt Modifieds, and Night 2 on Saturday adds Lightning Sprints as well. The track lists Sunday, Nov. 22, as a rain date. Its official ticketing sheet lists Thursday, Nov. 19, as an off day, with no practice or calcutta.

Clay-Per-View is offering a superpass covering the prelude and both championship nights. The stream is sold separately from the service’s regular on-demand subscription.
