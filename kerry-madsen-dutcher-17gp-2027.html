Kerry Madsen Lands Full-Time 2027 High Limit Ride in Dutcher #17GP

Madsen, who finished 2026 in the car, will run the full 2027 High Limit season in the #17GP.

Published October 30, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Kerry Madsen will drive the Michael Dutcher Motorsports #17GP full-time on the High Limit Racing tour in 2027.

Madsen began 2026 in the Vermeer Motorsports No. 55, but Vermeer moved Brady Bacon into that car late in the season and has named Bacon its 2027 driver. Madsen moved over to the Dutcher #17GP and finished the 2026 season in it.

Michael Dutcher Motorsports joined High Limit full-time in 2026 and is chasing High Roller Club membership.
