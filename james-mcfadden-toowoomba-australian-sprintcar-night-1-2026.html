James McFadden Wins Australian Sprintcar Championship Night 1 at Toowoomba

McFadden beat Ryan Newton by 1.03 seconds in Friday’s Preliminary A Main at Hi-Tec Oils Toowoomba Speedway. Brock Hallett finished third. Lachlan McHugh leads the points into Saturday’s finale.

Published November 21, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

James McFadden won the Preliminary A Main on Night 1 of the 64th Nutrend Homes Australian Sprintcar Championship on Friday, Nov. 20, at Hi-Tec Oils Toowoomba Speedway in Toowoomba, Queensland, according to Speedcafe. McFadden, driving the WX23, beat Ryan Newton (Q66) by 1.03 seconds. Brock Hallett (Q5) finished third, Lachlan McHugh (NQ7) fourth and Liam Williams (NQ26) fifth. Speedcafe reported McFadden is chasing a fourth Australian title, and that this is the first time Toowoomba has hosted the championship.

McHugh started from the pole and led most of the race, Speedcafe said. He had posted quick time in qualifying at 11.135 seconds and won his first heat. The yellow came out after one lap when Preliminary B Main winner Marcus Dumesny made contact with Henry Titman in turn 1, slowing up and catching Kaiden Manders. After a restart, the race was stopped when Randy Morgan spun in turn 4 and Brendan Quinn’s car caught fire in the paddock. McHugh led again with 12 laps to go before Jy Corbet found the wall in turn 4, climbed the concrete and flipped, collecting Matthew Dumesny, according to the report.

With six laps remaining, McFadden passed McHugh on the inside into turn 4. Speedcafe said McHugh’s car then tightened on corner exit and moved toward the inside wall, and that Hallett and Newton joined the fight as McHugh dropped from the lead to fourth in the space of a lap. Newton had charged from outside the top 10. The race ran green for the final 11 laps. Luke Oldfield (Q17) finished sixth, Aaron Kelly (Q7) seventh and Dumesny (N47) eighth. Reigning champion Jock Goodyer (A1), who transferred from the B Main and raced forward from the rear of the A Main, finished ninth after circulating with a flattening left rear tire under the last caution. Harry Stewart (Q96) was 10th.

McHugh leads the Night 1 points with 809, five ahead of Hallett (804), Speedcafe reported. McFadden and Williams are tied for third with 796, followed by Oldfield (783) and Kelly (772). The top six in points after Night 1 advance directly to the qualifying dash for the A Main and do not have to run further heat races. The championship finale is scheduled for Saturday, Nov. 21, at Toowoomba.

Sources: McFadden charges to win in thrilling Aussie Sprintcar title opener, Jack Fletcher, Speedcafe (Nov. 21, 2026); Night 1 points and qualifying report, Jack Fletcher, Speedcafe (Nov. 21, 2026). No permitted photo was available from this race.
