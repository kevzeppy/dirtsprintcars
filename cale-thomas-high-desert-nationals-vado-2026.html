Cale Thomas Wins $10,000 High Desert Nationals at Vado Speedway Park

Thomas started sixth and beat Caleb Saiz, who started 15th, in the 30-lap season finale at Vado, N.M., on Saturday, Nov. 14.

Published November 16, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Cale Thomas won the 30-lap High Desert Nationals 360 winged sprint car feature at Vado Speedway Park in Vado, New Mexico, on Saturday, Nov. 14, SPEED SPORT reported. The race paid $10,000 to win. Thomas started sixth in the No. 6. Caleb Saiz finished second after starting 15th.

Polesitter Ashton Torgerson finished third, Justin Sanders fourth and J.J. Hickle fifth. Elijah Gile, Brady Baker, Ryder Laplante, Braden Chiaramonte and John Carney II completed the top 10 in the 24-car feature, according to the published finish. Ryder McCutcheon, the 2026 POWRi 410 Outlaw Sprint League champion, started seventh and did not finish. Wyatt Miller, Peter Smith, Daniel Blaeser, Dylan Harris, Tyler Congleton and Vance Wofford also failed to finish.

The race was the second night of the Gaerte Engines 360 Winged Sprint Series season finale at Vado, according to the track’s event schedule. Hoseheads.com posted the same finish and listed Thomas as the winner of the $10,000 season finale.

Sources: It’s Cale Thomas In High Desert Nationals, SPEED SPORT (Nov. 15, 2026); Hoseheads.com results (Nov. 14, 2026); Upcoming Events, Vado Speedway Park.
