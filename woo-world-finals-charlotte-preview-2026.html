Nov 3: Gravel Chases Historic Three-Peat as WoO World Finals Open at Charlotte

David Gravel and Big Game Motorsports hold a 124-point lead over Michael Kofoid heading into the Dave Smith Motors World of Outlaws World Finals (Nov. 4-7) at The Dirt Track at Charlotte. Only Kinser, Schatz and Sweet have pulled off the three-peat trick.

Published November 3, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

All dirt roads lead to Charlotte this week as the World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Cars wrap season 49. Stout rosters are expected, with the usual suspects plus a few wild cards packing the pits. Gravel's consistency has him knocking on the door of exclusive company — the three-peat club is small, and the entry fee is measured in nights of sliding around the dirt without blinking.

Thursday through Saturday features will decide it, with hot laps and qualifying kicking things off Wednesday. If Gravel seals it, Big Game can start practicing their three-peat victory doughnuts. If Kofoid steals it, someone owes the points sheet an apology.
