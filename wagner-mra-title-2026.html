Nov 2: Jack Wagner Wins 2026 USAC MRA Sprint Car Title

"Gentleman Jack" Wagner dominated the USAC Wholesale Batteries Midwest Racing Association Sprint Car season with seven wins.

Published November 2, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Lone Jack, Missouri's Jack Wagner put together the best season of his career in a non-wing sprint car. Driving the Jesse Hockett/Wagner Boys Racing No. 77 "The Crow," he won the 2026 USAC MRA Sprint Car championship by 125 points over Chase Howard.

Wagner collected seven victories, four runner-up finishes and a fifth in 12 starts. That's the kind of consistency that makes the rest of the field look for other hobbies. His previous seasons included a 2021 Weld Memorial win and a 2023 POWRi WAR title, but 2026 was the full package.

Wyatt Burks finished third, with Samuel Wagner (Jack's brother) fourth and Braydon Cromwell fifth. Non-wing racing keeps delivering clean, hard-fought championships, and Wagner just added another one to the list.
