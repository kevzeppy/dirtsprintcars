Sam Hafertepe Jr. Wins Record Seventh ASCS National Tour Title

Hafertepe clinched the 2026 title Oct. 17 by winning the Fuzzy’s Fall Fling finale at Creek County Speedway with a last-lap pass. He beat Matt Covington by 41 points. After the season, he announced he will join Jess Jordan Motorsports in 2027 and shift his focus to selected 410 World of Outlaws and High Limit starts.

Published November 12, 2026 · Updated November 13, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Sam Hafertepe Jr. of Sunnyvale, Texas, won the 2026 American Sprint Car Series National Tour championship, his seventh series title and the most in ASCS history. He clinched it Oct. 17 at Creek County Speedway in Sapulpa, Oklahoma, by winning the 40-lap Fuzzy’s Fall Fling season finale with a pass for the lead on the last lap, ASCS reported.

Hafertepe entered the finale with a 21-point lead over Matt Covington and started eighth. He passed Covington for third with 14 laps left, got by Alex Sewell for second with two laps to go and took the lead from Garrett Benson in Turns 3 and 4 on the final lap. Benson finished second, Sewell third, Covington fourth and Steven Shebester fifth. Hafertepe won the title by 41 points over Covington.

The win was Hafertepe’s seventh of 2026 and the 95th ASCS National Tour feature win of his career. He made 27 feature starts this season with 19 top-five and 21 top-10 finishes, according to ASCS season statistics. His first five titles came in consecutive seasons from 2016 to 2020, and his sixth, in 2025, moved him past Jason Johnson for the most in series history. Hafertepe and car owner Miles Hill won both the driver and owner championships with the Hill’s Racing Team No. 15H.

“I owe it all to Miles and this team,” Hafertepe said at the ASCS awards banquet Oct. 18 in Tulsa. “For the last nine years, you’ve given us everything we need. This team never wavered this entire year, and as a driver, it’s the best kind of ownership you can have.”

Covington finished second in points and Seth Bergman third. Hafertepe collected $40,000 from the series points fund. The 2027 ASCS National Tour season opens Feb. 4-6 at Volusia Speedway Park during the DIRTcar Nationals.

After the season, a Jess Jordan Motorsports press release dated Oct. 20 said Hafertepe will join the newly formed, Texas-based team for 2027, concluding a partnership of nearly a decade with Hill’s Racing. The release said he will shift his focus from 360 to 410 sprint car racing and that his schedule will include selected World of Outlaws and High Limit events, with 2027 serving as a building year toward the long-term goal of competing on a national tour. Further details on the 2027 schedule will be announced later, the release said. Hafertepe posted the release on Oct. 21.

Brody Brown, who was Hafertepe’s head mechanic this past season at 17 years old, will join him at Jess Jordan Motorsports, the release said. The partnership with Miles Hill and Hill’s Racing produced five ASCS National Tour championships and more than 80 feature victories, according to the release.
