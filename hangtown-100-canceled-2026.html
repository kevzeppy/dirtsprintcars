Storms Cancel Hangtown 100 at Placerville; USAC Midgets Resume Nov. 18 at Plaza Park

Officials called off both nights, Nov. 13-14, because of a forecast for heavy rain. The decision ends the 2026 season at Placerville Speedway.

Published November 12, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Hangtown 100 officials canceled both nights of the event, Friday and Saturday, Nov. 13-14, at Placerville Speedway in Placerville, California, because of the weather forecast, TJ Slideways reported. Tickets will be refunded automatically, less credit card processing fees, and the money is expected in fans’ accounts within 7 to 10 days. No makeup date was announced.

“With how the forecast looks for this weekend we wanted to make an early decision since people are traveling from all over,” promoter Scott Russell said. He added that some forecast models showed up to about two inches of rain from Friday into Saturday. The cancellation ends the season at Placerville Speedway.

The Hangtown 100 was to open the USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midgets’ seven-race Golden State Invasion and include winged 360 sprint cars. Kyle Larson, a co-promoter of the event with Russell Motorsports, had been announced for both classes. The 2024 Hangtown 100 was also canceled, because of rain and cold, and Daison Pursley won the most recent running, in 2025.

USAC’s National Midgets resume Nov. 18 at Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia, according to USAC. The series then runs two nights at Merced Speedway on Nov. 20-21 and the Jason Leffler Memorial at Bakersfield on Nov. 24.

Sources: TJ Slideways; USAC (headline and midget schedule); USAC 2026 midget schedule.
