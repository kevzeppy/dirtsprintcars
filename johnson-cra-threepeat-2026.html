Oct 30: Johnson 3-peats as USAC CRA champ

#1 Again! Kevin Johnson made it three straight in the CRA series. The number on the car is starting to look permanent.

Published October 31, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Kevin Johnson secured his third consecutive USAC CRA Sprint Car championship in 2026. The non-wing series wrapped with Johnson on top once more.

Consistency and speed carried the day across the schedule. Another title for the veteran as the offseason silly season begins heating up.
