Nov 12: 2027 World of Outlaws Sprint Car Pennsylvania Dates Finalized

The World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series has finalized eight rounds in Pennsylvania for 2027, continuing the rivalry with the PA Posse.

The schedule opens May 12 at Lincoln Speedway for the Gettysburg Clash, followed by the Morgan Cup at Williams Grove May 14-15, Bricker’s Bash at BAPS July 29, Summer Nationals at Williams Grove July 30-31, and the National Open Oct. 1-2.
