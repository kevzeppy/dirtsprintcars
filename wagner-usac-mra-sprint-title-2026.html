Jack Wagner Wins 2026 USAC Wholesale Batteries MRA Sprint Car Title

Driving the Jesse Hockett/Wagner Boys Racing No. 77, Wagner won seven of 12 series starts and finished 125 points ahead of Chase Howard.

Published November 10, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Jack Wagner of Lone Jack, Missouri won the 2026 USAC Wholesale Batteries Midwest Racing Association Sprint Car championship. In the Jesse Hockett/Wagner Boys Racing No. 77, known as “The Crow,” Wagner posted seven victories, four second-place finishes and a fifth in 12 series starts and defeated Chase Howard of Nesbit, Mississippi by 125 points, 1,769-1,654.

Wagner also led the series with 172 laps led, six heat-race wins, and top-five and top-10 finishes in all 12 of his starts. He won three times at Grain Valley, Missouri’s Valley Speedway, in June, August and October’s John Helm Memorial, added a pair of victories at Butler, Missouri’s Electric City Speedway in May and July, and also won in July at Kansas City, Kansas’ Lakeside Speedway and Winston, Missouri’s I-35 Speedway. USAC noted it was his best season driving a non-winged sprint car. He won more races across all divisions in 2026 (10) than he had in the previous five seasons combined (8).

Ricky Lewis won two of his three series starts, including April’s season opener at Springfield Raceway on a last-lap pass of Braydon Cromwell. Wyatt Burks passed Wagner with two laps remaining to win August’s Weld Memorial at Lakeside. Tom Curran collected his first career sprint car win after passing Wagner in June at Valley, and Wesley Smith passed Wagner with two laps remaining to win in May at Valley.

Burks finished third in the standings, just ahead of Wagner’s brother, Samuel Wagner, with Cromwell fifth. Ten drivers started all 12 points-paying features, including both Wagners, Howard, Burks and Cromwell.

Sources: USAC press release by Ray Cunningham, November 2, 2026; photo: John Lee / USAC. USAC Racing: 2026 MRA Sprint Car season review.
