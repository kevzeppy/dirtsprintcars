Thursday World Finals Program Moved Up 1 Hour

To dodge a potential rain threat, the Dave Smith Motors World of Outlaws World Finals shifted Thursday’s action an hour earlier at The Dirt Track at Charlotte.

Published November 5, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Mother Nature tried to crash the party, so the Outlaws did what any good dirt crew would: they moved the start time. Opening ceremonies for Thursday’s portion of the World Finals now kick off at 2:50 p.m. ET, with racing following at 3 p.m. Friday and Saturday stay on the original schedule.

The move keeps the full program intact for the NOS Energy Drink Sprint Cars, Late Models and Super DIRTcar Series. Fans and teams get their laps without the weather gods stealing the show. Abreu’s quick-time sweep already set the stage; now the heats just start a little earlier so the dirt stays dry enough to race.

Source: World of Outlaws (Nick Graziano, Nov. 4, 2026).
