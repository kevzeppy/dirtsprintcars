Oct 20: IndyCar winner Rasmussen set for USAC midget debut at Turkey Night

Source: USAC Racing

NTT IndyCar Series race winner Christian Rasmussen will make his USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midget debut at the 85th Annual Turkey Night Grand Prix on November 28 at Ventura Raceway. He'll drive the No. 98 Agajanian Special for Johnny Cofer in a livery paying tribute to Troy Ruttman's 1952 Indy 500 winner.

Rasmussen called it "completely out of my wheelhouse," which is the polite way of saying "I've never slid a midget through a dirt corner while trying not to eat a fence." Practice is Friday the 27th; the 98-lapper pays $15k to win. Bring a turkey sandwich—or at least a good excuse for why you're still at the track on Thanksgiving weekend.
