Crown Jewels

Every winner of sprint car and midget racing's five crown jewel events, with A-main finishing orders.

Crown jewels: Knoxville Nationals, Kings Royal, National Open, Chili Bowl and Australian Classic (Grand Annual Sprintcar Classic, Warrnambool). See also Legends and This Day.

Knoxville Nationals

Knoxville Raceway, Knoxville, Iowa. First run in 1961 as the Super Modified National Championship; the Knoxville Nationals name dates to 1978. Run in August on the half-mile.
