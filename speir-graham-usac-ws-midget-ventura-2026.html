Speir Scores First Win, Graham Clinches First Title in Ventura USAC WS Midget Finale

Connor Speir led all 25 laps from the pole for his first career USAC Avanti Windows & Doors Western States Midget victory. Broedy Graham finished fourth to lock up his first series championship and the Billy’s Tequila Cup.

Published November 8, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

It was a night of firsts at Ventura Raceway. Connor Speir of Fillmore, California, started on the pole in the Matt Streeter-Graunstadt Enterprises No. 14 and led every lap of the 25-lap feature to claim his first USAC Western States Midget win by 0.495 seconds over Yantis and Culver.

Broedy Graham of Bakersfield crossed the line in fourth in his BGR Racing entry, enough to secure his first career USAC Western States Midget driving championship and the entrant title for his team. Graham also claimed the Billy’s Tequila Cup for the three Ventura events of the season.

The win capped the 2026 USAC Western States Midget season at the 1/5-mile Southern California dirt oval.

Source: SC Motorsports Media / Richie Murray – USAC Media (Nov. 7, 2026).
