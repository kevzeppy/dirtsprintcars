Nov 3: High Limit International Returns to Perth with $120k-to-Win Finale

High Limit Racing heads back to Perth Motorplex December 28-30 for the third annual International event, with $20,000-to-win prelims and a record $120,000 AUD to win the finale — the biggest payday in Australian sprint car history.

Published November 3, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

After Larson went back-to-back the previous two years, the series is bringing the highest-paying show Down Under yet. Sellout crowds expected on Kwinana Beach. If you're packing for Perth, bring sunscreen, a wing, and a plan for that six-figure check — the Aussies don't mess around when the purse is that fat.

Sources: High Limit Racing · Perth Motorplex · Dirt Track Digest
