Weekly preview

This week: World of Outlaws World Finals decide the title at Charlotte

The World of Outlaws close 2026 in Concord, POWRi crowns its 410 champion at Texas Motor Speedway, and several regional series run season finales.

Published November 1, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The schedule for Sunday, November 1 through Saturday, November 7, as published by the series and tracks. Check each track for start times, ticketing and weather before you go.

Wing sprint cars

Wednesday through Saturday, November 4–7, The Dirt Track at Charlotte, Concord, North Carolina. The Dave Smith Motors World of Outlaws World Finals close the 2026 World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car season, sharing the bill with the World of Outlaws Late Models and the Super DIRTcar Series. Wednesday is qualifying night. Thursday, Friday and Saturday each run a 25-lap sprint car feature, paying $15,000, $15,000 and $25,000 to win. Thursday’s program was moved up one hour because of a rain threat: opening ceremonies at 2:50 p.m. ET and racing at 3 p.m.; the midway Fan Zone still opens at 12 p.m. Friday and Saturday opening ceremonies remain at 4 p.m. ET. Source: World of Outlaws.

David Gravel leads the standings by 124 points over Michael “Buddy” Kofoid, 9,732 to 9,608, in the series’ last update on October 24. Sheldon Haudenschild is third, 222 back, and Carson Macedo is fourth, 320 back. Kofoid leads the series with 17 wins; Gravel and Haudenschild have 11 each.

Thursday through Saturday, November 5–7, Texas Motor Speedway Dirt Track, Fort Worth, Texas. The POWRi Honest Abe Roofing 410 Outlaw Sprint League closes its season at the second Deuces Wild Championships, with the series championship decided on the weekend. Thursday is time trials only, with grandstands closed. Friday pays $5,000 to win and Saturday pays $7,000, with $500 to start each night. The POWRi Heartland Late Model Tour and USRA American Racer Modified Series also finish their seasons there.

Friday and Saturday, November 20–21, Riverside International Speedway, West Memphis, Arkansas. The USCS Outlaw Thunder Tour runs the 18th annual Bobby Martin Memorial Flip Flop 50. Friday is Pole Night and Saturday is the finale. Editor’s note: This preview originally listed the event for Nov. 6–7. USCS postponed it to Nov. 20–21 because of the threat of high winds and unseasonably low temperatures in the Mid-South, and Riverside International Speedway lists the same new dates.

Saturday, November 7, Hendry County Motorsports Park, Clewiston, Florida. The Top Gun Sprint Series runs the Don Rehm Classic for 360 winged sprint cars, listed at $15,000 to win.

Saturday, November 7, Stockton Dirt Track, Stockton, California. The Sprint Car Challenge Tour 360s run the Tribute to Gary Patterson.

Non-wing sprint cars

The USAC CRA Sprint Car finale scheduled for Saturday, November 7 at Perris Auto Speedway has been canceled and will not be made up. R.J. Johnson won his third straight USAC CRA title; the season ended October 24 at Central Arizona Raceway.

On Saturday, November 7, the Hunt Wingless Spec Sprints run their championship final at Stockton. The Ultimate Sprint Car Series 360 non-wing cars are listed at Santa Maria Raceway on Friday and Ventura Raceway on Saturday.

Midgets and USAC

The USAC National Sprint Car and USAC Silver Crown seasons are complete; Kyle Cummins clinched his second straight National Sprint Car title in Arizona. USAC National Midgets have no dates this week and return at Placerville Speedway for the Hangtown 100 next weekend.

Australia and New Zealand

Saturday, November 7, Bunbury Speedway, Western Australia. The WA Sprintcar Championship, round 3 of the Maddington Toyota Sprintcar Series.

Saturday, November 7, Avalon Raceway, Lara, Victoria. The Routley’s Bakery Sprintcar Fireball Derby, which is also round 2 of the track championship.

Saturday, November 7, Baypark Speedway, Tauranga, New Zealand. Sprintcars race with the Bill Buckley Midget Car Classic. Sunday, November 8 is the rain date.

Looking ahead: the Australian Sprintcar Championship runs November 18–21 at Toowoomba Speedway in Queensland, with November 22 as the rain date.
