Nov 2: Wagner takes 2026 USAC MRA Sprint Car title

Jack Wagner claimed the 2026 USAC Wholesale Batteries Midwest Racing Association Sprint Car championship with seven wins in 12 starts.

Published November 5, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Jack Wagner put in the work and walked away with the 2026 USAC MRA Sprint Car title. Driving the Jesse Hockett/Wagner Boys Racing No. 77 "The Crow," the Lone Jack, Missouri driver posted seven wins, four seconds, and a fifth across 12 series starts, beating Chase Howard by 125 points.

It was his best season yet in non-winged sprints. Previous highlights include the 2021 Weld Memorial and 2023 POWRi WAR championship. The MRA trail mixed regular points races with a few non-points specials, including a dramatic final-lap pass by Jadon Rogers at Red Hill.

Wagner's consistency turned a solid year into a championship. Sometimes the crow flies high.
