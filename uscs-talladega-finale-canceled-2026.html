USCS Cancels 30th Anniversary Finale at Talladega Short Track

Both nights of the scheduled Nov. 13-14 championship weekend at the Hornet’s Nest are listed as canceled. No makeup date has been posted.

Published November 13, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The United Sprint Car Series has canceled both nights of its 30th anniversary championship weekend, Friday and Saturday, Nov. 13-14, at Talladega Short Track in Eastaboga, Alabama. The official USCS schedule lists the Friday and Saturday programs as canceled, and Talladega Short Track’s schedule marks the same two nights canceled.

Neither the series nor the track has published a reason for the cancellation in those official schedule listings, and neither lists a makeup date. Talladega Short Track TV also lists both of its planned broadcasts from the weekend as canceled. The track’s next published event is the 3rd Annual Santa Shootout on Saturday, Dec. 19, which does not include USCS.

The weekend had been listed as the series’ 30th anniversary championship finale at the track known as the Hornet’s Nest. USCS still lists the ACE DORAN 18th Annual Bobby Martin Memorial Flip Flop 50 for Friday and Saturday, Nov. 20-21, at Riverside International Speedway in West Memphis, Arkansas.

Sources: USCS schedule, Nov. 13 at Talladega Short Track; USCS schedule, Nov. 14 at Talladega Short Track; Talladega Short Track schedule; Talladega Short Track TV.
