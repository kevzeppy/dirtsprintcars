Kyle Cummins Seeking Ownership Partners for 2027 USAC Return

The two-time and defending USAC AMSOIL National Sprint Car champion is pursuing ownership and partnership options to keep the championship-winning team together after Petty Performance Racing ends its national program.

Published November 6, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Kyle Cummins is pursuing ownership and partnership opportunities to keep his championship-winning national sprint car team intact and return to the USAC AMSOIL Sprint Car National Tour in 2027, according to a Nov. 5, 2026 Kyle Cummins Racing update. The move follows Petty Performance Racing’s decision to conclude its involvement in the national sprint car program after back-to-back titles with Cummins.

Jerry Petty and his wife, Bev, are stepping away from the national program to devote more time to family. In a post-season interview with USAC Racing, Petty said the future of the USAC National team would be up to Cummins, and that Petty Performance Racing would not continue the national program into 2027. USAC’s 2026 season review similarly reported that the Petty Performance Racing national team was shutting down and would not continue next year. Petty has entrusted Cummins with guiding the team’s next chapter.

Cummins, of Princeton, Indiana, won the 2025 and 2026 USAC AMSOIL National Sprint Car titles in the Petty Performance Racing No. 3p. In 2026 he led the series with 11 wins, 351 laps led, 35 top-fives and 45 top-10s in 48 feature starts, according to USAC. Across the two-year run with Petty Performance Racing, Cummins recorded 21 wins, 73 top-fives and 89 top-10s in 97 starts.

The Nov. 5 update outlines a plan to preserve the winning formula: retain core crew members Cameron LaRose, Kasey DeRenzy and Zach Fields, with Cummins continuing as the driver. Those three were identified by USAC as core team members during the 2026 championship season. Possible paths include a complete team acquisition, a new ownership structure, strategic investment or long-term corporate sponsorship; terms were described as open. No contact information is published here because a public copy of the full release with inquiry details could not be independently located for verification.
