David Gravel Clinches Third Straight World of Outlaws Championship

David Gravel finished sixth in Thursday’s World Finals feature at The Dirt Track at Charlotte to clinch his third consecutive World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series title.

Published November 6, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

David Gravel clinched his third consecutive World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series championship Thursday night at the Dave Smith Motors World of Outlaws World Finals at The Dirt Track at Charlotte in Concord, N.C. Gravel, driving the Big Game Motorsports No. 2, started fifth and finished sixth in the 25-lap feature won by Rico Abreu. That finish locked up the title.

Gravel is only the fourth driver in series history to win three straight World of Outlaws championships, joining Steve Kinser, Donny Schatz and Brad Sweet. The title is the third of his career, which ties him with Sammy Swindell for the fourth-most championships in series history. Gravel and Big Game Motorsports won their first title together in 2024 and repeated in 2025.

Gravel entered the World Finals with a 124-point lead over Michael “Buddy” Kofoid, according to official World of Outlaws standings. Before Thursday’s feature, Gravel posted on social media that a top-16 finish would secure the championship. Abreu won Thursday’s feature ahead of Tyler Courtney and Bill Balog, with Kofoid fourth and Garet Williamson fifth.

The World Finals continue Friday and conclude Saturday at The Dirt Track at Charlotte, closing the 2026 World of Outlaws season.
