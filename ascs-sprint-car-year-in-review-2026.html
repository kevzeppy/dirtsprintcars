2026 American Sprint Car Series Year in Review

Sam Hafertepe Jr. secured a record seventh ASCS National Tour championship while three first-time winners joined the series history books.

Published November 25, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The American Sprint Car Series published its 2026 year-in-review on November 25, wrapping a 35th season that ran 28 races across 19 tracks and 11 states. Sam Hafertepe Jr. of Sunnyvale, Texas, claimed his seventh National Tour title in the Hill’s Racing No. 15H, setting a new series championship record.

Hafertepe recorded seven wins, including a last-lap pass of Garrett Benson and Alex Sewell to clinch the title at Creek County Speedway’s Fuzzy’s Fall Fling. The season also produced three first-time ASCS National Tour winners: Levi Hillier, Jett Barnes, and another breakout driver highlighted in the review.

Cole Schroeder earned Brodix Rookie of the Year honors in his first full national campaign. Regional champions were crowned across ASCS’ divisions, with the series looking ahead to a 2027 schedule that opens at Volusia Speedway Park.
