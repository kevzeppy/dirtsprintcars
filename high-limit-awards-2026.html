Oct 28: Abreu Crowned Back-to-Back High Limit Champ at Awards Celebration

Draft – pending Kevin's approval.

Rico Abreu didn't just win the 2026 Interstate Batteries High Limit title by 31 points over Gio Scelzi – he showed up to the Fort Worth Stockyards banquet and delivered two speeches that apparently had the room in stitches. First with TSR, first for Tony Stewart as an owner in the series.

Teams hauled home north of $3 million in postseason money. Tanner Holmes cleaned up in the awards department. Source: High Limit Racing / Dirt Track Digest, Oct 29, 2026.
