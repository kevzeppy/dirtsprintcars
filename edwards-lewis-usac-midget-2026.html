Oct 30: Edwards wins USAC States Midget finale; Lewis is champ

Edwards took the win at Kern while Lewis locked up the Western States title. Small cars, big celebrations.

Published October 31, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Drake Edwards won the USAC Western States Midget finale at Kern County Raceway, but Michael Lewis is the 2026 champion.

The series wrapped its season with the title fight decided on the track. Lewis held on for the crown while Edwards pocketed the finale win. Another solid year of tight racing in the little cars.
