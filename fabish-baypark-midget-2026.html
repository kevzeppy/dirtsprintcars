Mitch Fabish Wins Third Straight Baypark Midget Feature

Te Awamutu’s Mitch Fabish extended his unbeaten start to the New Zealand speedway season with a third consecutive Midget Car feature win, taking the Easysteel Bill Buckley Classic at Baypark Speedway by 0.608 seconds over Alec Insley.

Published November 9, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Mitch Fabish continues to dominate early in the 2026-27 New Zealand midget season. On Sunday night at Baypark Speedway, he held off Auckland teenager Alec Insley throughout a caution-free 25-lap feature to win the Easysteel Bill Buckley Classic.

The victory follows wins at Baypark’s Labour Weekend season opener and the Rosetown 40-lapper at Kihikihi the previous weekend. Fabish’s early form has him firmly in the conversation as the New Zealand Midget Championship approaches.

In the Sprint Car feature the same night, former New Zealand champion Jonathan Allard led from start to finish for his second win of the season.

Sources: SunLive (Nov. 9, 2026); VelocityNews.
