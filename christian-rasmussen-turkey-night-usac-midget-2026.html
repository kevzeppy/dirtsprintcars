IndyCar Winner Christian Rasmussen to Make USAC Midget Debut at Turkey Night

The Danish driver will race the No. 98 Agajanian Special for Johnny Cofer and Dan Esslinger in the 85th Turkey Night Grand Prix, the USAC National Midget season finale, on Nov. 28 at Ventura Raceway.

Published November 12, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

NTT IndyCar Series race winner Christian Rasmussen will make his USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midget debut in the 85th ARP Turkey Night Grand Prix on Saturday, Nov. 28, at Ventura Raceway in Ventura, California. The 26-year-old from Copenhagen, Denmark, drives the No. 21 Chevrolet for Ed Carpenter Racing in IndyCar, where he won for the first time at the Milwaukee Mile on Aug. 24, 2025.

Rasmussen will drive the No. 98, a Spike short-arm chassis with an SR-11 engine fielded by car owner Johnny Cofer. With support from the Agajanian family, the car will carry a paint scheme based on the 1952 Agajanian Special that Troy Ruttman drove to victory in the Indianapolis 500. The entry reunites Cofer and Dan Esslinger, who won the 1994 USAC Western States Midget championship together with Cofer driving and Esslinger as owner. Cofer's daughter, Maria Cofer, has raced the same car.

"I'm excited to try something that is completely out of my wheelhouse," Rasmussen said. "But most of all, I'm excited to get to work with Johnny Cofer and Dan Esslinger." Esslinger described it as a one-race deal for now.

Rasmussen won the USF2000 title in 2020, the Indy Pro 2000 title in 2021 and the INDY NXT title in 2023. He also won the LMP2 class at the 2024 Rolex 24 at Daytona. Practice for Turkey Night is Friday, Nov. 27. Qualifying, heat races and the 98-lap main event are Saturday, Nov. 28, and the race will stream on FloRacing.

Sources: USAC Racing (Jordan Agajanian, Oct. 20, 2026); SPEED SPORT; RACER.
