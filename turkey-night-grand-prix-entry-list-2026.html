54 Drivers on Turkey Night Grand Prix Entry List at Ventura Raceway

The historic Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura Raceway on Nov. 28 has drawn a deep 54-car field for the USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midget Championship finale of the Golden State Invasion.

Published November 17, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Fifty-four drivers are listed on the entry list for the Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura Raceway on Saturday, Nov. 28, USAC announced on Nov. 17.

The event closes the Golden State Invasion of California for the USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midget Championship. The series continues Friday and Saturday, Nov. 20-21, at Merced Speedway and Tuesday, Nov. 24, at Bakersfield Speedway at Kevin Harvick’s Kern Raceway before heading to Ventura.

USAC previously noted that IndyCar winner Christian Rasmussen is set to make his USAC midget debut at Turkey Night. Other notable entries continue to build for the traditional Thanksgiving weekend closer at the Ventura oval.

Details and updates are available on USAC’s website and social channels.

Sources: Wide Open! 54 Drivers on Turkey Night Grand Prix Entry List at Ventura, USAC Racing (Nov. 17, 2026).
