POWRi 410 Sprints Close 2026 Season at Texas Motor Speedway Dirt Track

The second Deuces Wild Championships runs Nov. 5-7 in Fort Worth, with two complete shows paying $5,000 and $7,000 to win and the POWRi 410 Outlaw Sprint League title on the line.

Published November 4, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The POWRi Honest Abe Roofing 410 Outlaw Sprint League wraps up its 2026 season this weekend at the Dirt Track at Texas Motor Speedway in Fort Worth, Texas, in the second running of the Deuces Wild Championships. The event also serves as the points finale for the POWRi Heartland Late Model Tour and the USRA American Racer Modified Series.

New for 2026, time trials and hot laps for all three divisions move to Thursday, Nov. 5, with grandstands closed and racing beginning at 6:30 p.m. local time. Friday, Nov. 6 and Saturday, Nov. 7 are separate complete shows that go straight to heat races. Friday racing is set for 7 p.m.; Saturday starts a half-hour earlier, with racing estimated at 6:30 p.m. The 410 winged sprint cars race for $5,000 to win and $500 to start on Friday, and $7,000 to win and $500 to start on Saturday. Late Models pay $3,000 to win nightly and Modifieds $2,000, with about $110,000 in total purses across the weekend.

The expected 410 field listed by event organizers includes Ryder McCutcheon (R), Joe B. Miller, Xavier Doney, Ayrton Gennetten, Landon Crawley, Gage Montgomery, Miles Paulus, Dylan Bloomfield, John Barnard, Scotty Milan, Natalie Doney, Kyle Bellm, Howard Moore, Roger Crockett, Joel Myers Jr., Blake Hahn, Jace Park, Colton Fisher (R), Kyle Clark, Jack Potter, Kaleb Johnson, Cam Sorrels, Kameron Key, Austin McCarl, Sam Hafertepe Jr., Justin Henderson, Austin Mundie, Weston Gorham, Ryan Padgett, Brenham Crouch, Rees Moran, Landon Thompson and Eric Baldaccini. Driver registration is free, and 120 paved pit spaces will be assigned first-come, first-served on Thursday.

The National Weather Service forecast at the time of the event release called for sunny, dry conditions with highs in the 70s each day. A two-day adult grandstand pass is $53.98, and fan parking in the paved lot at the dirt track entrance is free. Start2Finish (S2FTV) will stream the event live. DirtSprintCars.com will post results after each night.

Sources: POWRi Texas Events Info Hub (DarinShort.com), event release Nov. 2, 2026; POWRi.
