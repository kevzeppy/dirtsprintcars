Briggs Danner Parts Ways with Hogue Racing After 2026

Danner is out of the No. 39 and on the open market for 2027.

Published October 30, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Briggs Danner and Hogue Racing Enterprises are parting ways after the 2026 USAC AMSOIL National Sprint Car season. Danner announced the split via social media.

Danner drove the Hogue No. 39 on the USAC national tour, and the pairing produced wins in 2026, including a wire-to-wire Thursday win at Eldora in September.

As of now, Danner is a free agent heading into 2027. Car owners with an open seat, your phone may be about to ring.

Sources: USAC: Danner’s Sept. 24 Eldora win in the Hogue No. 39 · Split first reported by Always Race Day and announced on Danner’s social media
