Rico Abreu Sweeps World Finals Qualifying Night at Charlotte

Rico Abreu set overall Simpson Quick Time for Thursday and Friday heat lineups Wednesday night at the Dave Smith Motors World of Outlaws World Finals at The Dirt Track at Charlotte.

Published November 5, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

More than 40 competitors signed in for the World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series season sendoff at The Dirt Track at Charlotte. Wednesday’s Honest Abe Roofing Qualifying sessions set heat race lineups for Thursday and Friday. Feature points accumulated over those two nights will set heats for Saturday’s $25,000-to-win season finale.

David Gravel topped Flight A for Thursday’s program in the Big Game Motorsports No. 2 with a 12.688. Rico Abreu, in the Tony Stewart/Curb-Agajanian Racing No. 14, set overall Simpson Quick Time with a 12.584 in Flight B. Bill Balog, Buddy Schweibinz, Tyler Courtney and Anthony Macri completed Thursday Flight A’s top five. Michael “Buddy” Kofoid, Garet Williamson, Tanner Holmes and Sheldon Haudenschild rounded out Flight B’s top five behind Abreu.

Abreu also paced Friday Flight A with a 12.542, sweeping both Simpson Quick Time awards. Sheldon Haudenschild led Friday Flight B at 12.678 in the KCP Racing/NOS Energy Drink Grand Prix Guava No. 18. Buddy Schweibinz, Bill Balog, Giovanni Scelzi and Parker Price-Miller completed Friday Flight A’s top five after Abreu. Cameron Smith, Tanner Holmes, Matt Farnham and Carson Macedo rounded out Flight B behind Haudenschild.

Separately, World of Outlaws announced that Thursday’s World Finals program has been moved up one hour because of a rain threat. Opening ceremonies are scheduled for 2:50 p.m. ET, with racing at 3 p.m. Friday and Saturday programs remain unchanged.

Sources: World of Outlaws (Alex Nieten, Nov. 4, 2026); World of Outlaws (Thursday schedule move).
