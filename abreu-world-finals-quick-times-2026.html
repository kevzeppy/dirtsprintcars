Nov 5: Abreu sweeps Quick Times at World Finals Qualifying Night

Rico Abreu set overall Simpson Quick Time in both sessions at The Dirt Track at Charlotte, posting a 12.542 for Friday's program.

Published November 5, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Rico Abreu didn't just show up to Charlotte—he brought a stopwatch and a broom. On World Finals Qualifying Night, the Tony Stewart/Curb-Agajanian Racing driver swept the Simpson Quick Time awards.

He ran a 12.584 in Thursday's Flight B to take overall QT, then backed it up with a 12.542 in Friday's Flight A. David Gravel topped Thursday's Flight A as he chases a third straight World of Outlaws title. Michael Kofoid, Garet Williamson, Sheldon Haudenschild and others filled the top fives across the flights.

Wednesday's times set heat lineups for Thursday and Friday. Points from those nights will set Saturday's $25,000-to-win season finale heats. Over 40 cars signed in for the NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series sendoff.
