Nov 4: USAC drops 29-race 2026 National Midget schedule

The 2026 USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midget Championship schedule features 29 races, highlighted by the seven-event Golden State Invasion in November.

Published November 5, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

USAC just dropped the 2026 National Midget schedule, and it's got 29 races on the docket. The Golden State Invasion closes the year with seven events across California, starting with the Hangtown 100 at Placerville Speedway on Nov. 13-14.

Other stops include Plaza Park Raceway (Visalia, Nov. 18), Merced Speedway (Nov. 20-21), Bakersfield Speedway (Jason Leffler Memorial, Nov. 24), and the Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura Raceway (Nov. 28). Corey Day is already confirmed for all seven in Willie Kahne's car.

Earlier portions of the trail include Indiana Midget Week, Mid-America stops, and the 4-Crown Nationals at Eldora. Full details are on the USAC site—because nothing says "plan your winter" like a 29-race midget calendar.
