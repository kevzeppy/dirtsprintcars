Kalib Henry Joins Beaver Racing for 2027 POWRi 410 Season

The 27-year-old from Sacramento, California, will drive the No. 12X Maxim powered by Don Ott Racing Engines. Henry is leaving Jim Muth’s Racers for Life No. 101 after back-to-back All Star Circuit of Champions titles.

Published November 18, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Kalib Henry will join Beaver Racing beginning in 2027, according to a release posted Nov. 18 on Kalib Henry Racing’s website. The 27-year-old from Sacramento, California, will drive the No. 12X Maxim powered by Don Ott Racing Engines. Henry and Beaver Racing will race for a POWRi 410 Outlaw Sprint League championship and in select World of Outlaws and High Limit Racing events, the release said. Henry is coming off his second consecutive All Star Circuit of Champions driver’s championship, which he clinched with a second-place finish in the season finale Oct. 3 at Michaels Mercer Raceway Park, according to SPEED SPORT.

Henry is leaving Jim Muth’s Racers for Life No. 101, which he first drove at the 2023 Knoxville Nationals and then full-time for the past three seasons, the release said. “I’m excited to join Beaver Racing for 2027,” Henry said. “Billy and Cheryl Vielhauer have been amazing to work with as everything began to fall into place. They have worked relentlessly to not only ensure Bob’s [Vielhauer] legacy lives on but to field a competitive team that can compete for wins at any level. I am honored they believe in me to drive their race car and excited to get to work and grow with them next year and beyond.”

In that span, Henry recorded 16 wins, 50 top-fives and 72 top-10s, according to the release. He won six features and the Fremont Speedway 410 track championship in 2024, then followed in 2025 with five wins and his first All Star driver’s championship. In 2026 he defended the All Star title with four wins and claimed the team’s first All Star owner’s championship, the release said. He also started the Kings Royal main event in 2026. His 2026 U.S. record was 37 races, four wins, 14 top-fives and 20 top-10s. Henry thanked Muth and crew chief Andy Potter in the announcement. “I want to personally thank Jim ‘Doc’ Muth for the opportunity to pilot the Racers for Life No. 101 starting at the 2023 Knoxville Nationals and then full-time for the last three years,” Henry said. “His commitment to the team’s success was pivotal, and I will forever be grateful for what we built together.”

Beaver Racing is a family-owned team based in Shawnee, Kansas, started in 1980 by Bob “Beaver” Vielhauer, a 2022 Knoxville Raceway Hall of Fame inductee. The operation is now led by Billy and Cheryl Vielhauer and their daughter Samantha, the release said. In 2026 the team recorded a victory, six top-fives and 11 top-10s competing with the POWRi 410 Outlaw Sprint League. “We’re excited to welcome Kalib to the team!” Beaver Racing said. “He has really proven himself through his success racing with the All Stars and has consistently shown the hard work and dedication it takes to compete. We can’t wait to see him bring that same work ethic, drive, and determination to the 12X. We’re looking forward to what the future holds and are excited to have him as part of the team!” The release said full details of team partners and the 2027 schedule will be announced later.

Sources: Kalib Henry Joins Beaver Racing for 2027, Kalib Henry Racing (Nov. 18, 2026); Flick Tops All Star Finale, Henry Is Champ, SPEED SPORT (Oct. 4, 2026). The 2025 All Star title is also confirmed by TJSlideways.com and Dirt Track Digest.
