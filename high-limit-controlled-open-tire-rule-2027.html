High Limit Adopts Controlled Open Tire Rule for 2027

Interstate Batteries High Limit Racing will allow teams to choose between designated Hoosier Racing Tire and American Racer compounds at series events next season.

Published November 20, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Interstate Batteries High Limit Racing will adopt a controlled open tire rule for the 2027 season, allowing teams to choose between and run designated Hoosier Racing Tire and American Racer compounds at series events, the series announced Friday from Mooresville, N.C. The change, published in a High Limit release, will be written into the official 2027 rulebook.

“Since the inception of High Limit our first and foremost objective has been the growth and health of Sprint Car Racing as an entirety,” said Brad Sweet, the six-time national champion who founded the series alongside three-time NASCAR Cup Series champion Kyle Larson in 2022. “This was not a decision that we took lightly, but after seeing and feeling the product in action and discussing this with teams, drivers, and vendors, we believe this is the best move for the sport moving forward. The opportunity is now there for both Hoosier Racing Tire and American Racer to create the best product possible and offer competitive prices. Teams are now afforded an extra option, and fans now have another storyline to follow each night and throughout the season.”

According to the release, only designated racing tires will be permitted on all four positions at High Limit Sprint Car Series events. Right rear tires must durometer at least 40. Left rear Hoosier tires must durometer at least 35, and left rear American Racers must durometer at least 38. The designated tires listed in the 2027 rulebook language are, for the front, the Hoosier 85/8.0-15 in D12, D15 and D20 and the American Racer 26.0/8.0-15DT in SD-33 and SD-38; for the left rear, the Hoosier 15.0/92-15, 15.0/93-15, 15.0/94-15, 15.0/95-15 and 15.0/96-15 in D12A and the American Racer 28.5/15.0-15BT, 29.0/15.0-15BT, 29.5/15.0-15BT, 30.0/15.0-15BT and 30.5/15.0-15BT in SD38; and for the right rear, the Hoosier 105/16.0-15 in D15A and Medium, the Hoosier 105/18.0-15 in D15A and RD20A, and the American Racer 34.0/17.0-15GT in SD40/MC2.

High Limit said Hoosier, owned by Continental, has been the predominant tire manufacturer in sprint car racing for the past 14 years and will remain an option. With American Racer also permitted, the series said 2027 will mark the first time since 2010-12, when Goodyear was used with the World of Outlaws, that a tire company other than Hoosier is allowed at the top level of the sport. Officials plan to work directly with national and local vendors for both brands to supply as much product as possible at sanctioned events, the release said.

Source: High Limit Makes Landmark Decision; Allowing American Racer and Hoosier with Controlled Open Tire Rule in 2027, High Limit Racing (Brian Walker, Nov. 20, 2026). Also reported by FloRacing.
