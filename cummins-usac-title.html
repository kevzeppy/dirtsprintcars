Oct 22: Cummins clinches second straight USAC National Sprint Car title

Source: USAC Racing

Kyle Cummins locked up his second consecutive USAC AMSOIL National Sprint Car championship at the Western World opener at Central Arizona Raceway with a third-place finish. Petty Performance Racing also secured the entrant title. Cummins finished the season with a comfortable points margin after a year of consistent top-10s.

Back-to-back titles are nice. Doing it while the rest of the field is still arguing about who has the best setup for Casa Grande is nicer. Congrats to the whole Petty Performance crew.
