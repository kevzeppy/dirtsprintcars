54 Drivers Entered for USAC Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura

USAC announced a wide-open 54-car field for the Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura Raceway on Nov. 28, the finale of the Golden State Invasion.

Published November 17, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

USAC released a 54-driver entry list for the Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura Raceway, set for Saturday, Nov. 28. The event closes the Golden State Invasion for the NOS Energy Drink National Midget Championship.

The list is wide open, with drivers from across the country entered for the traditional Thanksgiving weekend midget classic. Earlier in the invasion, Plaza Park was postponed and rescheduled to Nov. 23 due to rain, while Merced runs Nov. 20-21 and Bakersfield Nov. 24.

Full details and the entry list are available on the USAC website.

Source: USAC Racing News, USAC (Nov. 17, 2026). Additional reporting from series social channels.
