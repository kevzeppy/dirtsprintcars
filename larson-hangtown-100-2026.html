Nov 3: Kyle Larson Set for Double Duty at Hangtown 100

NASCAR star Kyle Larson returns to Placerville Speedway for USAC National Midgets and 360 Sprint Cars on Nov. 13-14.

Published November 3, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Kyle Larson is trading the Cup car for something a little smaller and a lot dirtier. The Elk Grove native will pull double duty at the Hangtown 100, racing both the USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midgets and the winged 360 Sprint Cars at Placerville Speedway.

Larson has a soft spot for the place. He made his first sprint car start there in 2007 and scored his first sprint car win the same season. He also won the midget portion of Hangtown in 2019 and 2023. Saturday's 100-lap midget feature pays $10,000 to win. The 360s pay $2,000 each afternoon.

It's a rare chance to see one of the best in the world on a tight bullring where he cut his teeth. The Golden State Invasion continues elsewhere on the midget schedule, but Hangtown is the headliner.
