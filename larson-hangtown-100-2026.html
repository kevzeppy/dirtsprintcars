Nov 3: Kyle Larson Double Duty at Hangtown 100 USAC Midgets and 360 Sprints

NASCAR star Kyle Larson returns to Placerville Speedway November 13-14 for the Hangtown 100, racing both the USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midgets (100-lap $10k-to-win Saturday) and Winged 360 Sprint Cars.

Published November 3, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Larson already owns Hangtown 100 midget wins from 2019 and 2023. He's also a co-promoter of the event, so if he wins again someone might need to check if the track is still level. Early 2 p.m. starts both days to beat the November cold — the dirt doesn't care that it's chilly, and neither does Larson when he's back in a midget.

Fans can catch it live on FloRacing. The California native made his first sprint car start at Placerville back in the day; now he's bringing the Cup Series point lead with him for a dirt double dip.
