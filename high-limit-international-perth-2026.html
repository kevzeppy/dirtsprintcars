High Limit International Returns to Perth Motorplex Dec. 28-30 with $120,000-to-Win Finale

The third annual event at Perth Motorplex in Kwinana Beach, Western Australia, features $20,000-to-win prelims and a record $120,000 AUD main event, the biggest payday in Australian sprint car history.

Published November 15, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

High Limit Racing returns to Western Australia for the third annual High Limit International at Perth Motorplex on December 28-30, 2026. The event will pay $20,000 to win each of the two preliminary nights and a record $120,000 AUD to win the finale, organizers said.

Kyle Larson won the first two editions of the event. The 2025 finale paid $110,000 AUD, according to previous reports. Tickets are on sale through the Perth Motorplex website, with 3-day reserved seating and silver terrace options available. Camping is also offered from Boxing Day through early January.

The series described the event as the biggest payday in Australian speedway history. Racing is scheduled to start around 6 p.m. local time each night, with the event available on FloRacing.

High Limit’s next U.S. action is expected in early 2027 after the international stop.
