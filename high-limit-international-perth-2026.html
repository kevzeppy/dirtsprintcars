High Limit International Returns to Perth Motorplex Dec. 28-30 With Record $120,000 to Win

The third annual event at Kwinana Beach, Western Australia, pays $20,000 to win each preliminary night and $120,000 to win the finale, a new Australian record, according to High Limit Racing.

Published November 15, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The third annual High Limit International will run Dec. 28, 29 and 30 at Perth Motorplex in Kwinana Beach, Western Australia, according to the High Limit Racing schedule. High Limit Racing said the event will pay $20,000 to win each preliminary night and $120,000 to win the main event, setting a new Australian record.

Kyle Larson has won both previous editions. In the 2025 finale on Dec. 30, Larson held off Dayne Kingshott to collect $110,000 AUD, which High Limit Racing called the richest payday in Australian speedway history at the time. Buddy Kofoid finished third, with Cole Macedo and Luke Oldfield completing the top five. High Limit announced the 2026 dates and purse that night.

Perth Motorplex said the first two editions drew sellout crowds. Three-day tickets are on sale through the track. Reserved seats on the front straight for all three nights cost $257 AUD for adults, $176 for ages 13 to 17 and $86 for ages 5 to 12. Three-day Silver passes, for the grassed terraces on the front and back straights, cost $203 for adults, $138 for youths and $70 for children and include pit access, according to the track’s event page.

Sources: 2026 High Limit International schedule, High Limit Racing; Larson Goes Back-to-Back in High Limit International, High Limit Racing (Dec. 30, 2025); High Limit International: Perth event page, Perth Motorplex.
