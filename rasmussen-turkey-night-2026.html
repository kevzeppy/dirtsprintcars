IndyCar winner Rasmussen set for USAC Midget debut at Turkey Night

Denmark's Christian Rasmussen trades the IndyCar for a midget at Ventura. Hope he packed his dirt shoes — Turkey Night doesn't care about open-wheel resumes.

Published October 21, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

NTT IndyCar Series race winner Christian Rasmussen will make his USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midget debut at the 85th Annual ARP Turkey Night Grand Prix on November 28 at Ventura Raceway.

He will drive a Spike chassis for Johnny Cofer in a special Agajanian Special paint scheme. Rasmussen called the opportunity "completely out of my wheelhouse" but expressed excitement about working with Cofer and Dan Esslinger.
