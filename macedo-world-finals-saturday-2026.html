Carson Macedo Wheels from Row Four to World Finals Finale Victory

Carson Macedo charged from eighth to win Saturday's $25,000 World of Outlaws season finale at The Dirt Track at Charlotte. Michael Kofoid finished second; Giovanni Scelzi was third. David Gravel is the 2026 champion.

Published November 8, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Carson Macedo and Jason Johnson Racing closed the 2026 World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series season in style. Starting eighth in the 25-lap, $25,000-to-win Dave Smith Motors World Finals feature at The Dirt Track at Charlotte, Macedo worked his way forward, took the lead with a slide job on Giovanni Scelzi on Lap 17, then held off a late challenge from Michael Kofoid to take the checkered flag by 0.324 seconds.

Scelzi, who had led the first 16 laps from the pole, finished third. Buddy Schweibinz and Steven Snyder Jr. completed the top five. David Gravel, who clinched his third consecutive championship earlier in the weekend, finished 17th.

It was Macedo's eighth World of Outlaws win of the season and the 66th of his career, tying him with Bobby Davis Jr. and Craig Dollansky for 14th all-time. It was also the 90th World of Outlaws victory for Jason Johnson Racing. Macedo called it a great way to end a season that didn't go exactly as planned for the team.

The World Finals wrapped a season that saw David Gravel become just the fourth driver to win three straight World of Outlaws Sprint Car titles.
