Daison Pursley Wins Winged 360 Feature at Merced Chase Johnson Classic

Pursley started fourth and won the 30-lap feature at Merced Speedway on Saturday, Nov. 21. Corey Day was second and polesitter Colby Copeland third.

Published November 22, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Daison Pursley won the 30-lap winged 360 sprint car feature at Merced Speedway’s Chase Johnson Classic in Merced, California, on Saturday, Nov. 21, according to results posted by Merced Speedway and a SPEED SPORT report. Pursley started fourth in the No. 87 on the quarter-mile track.

Corey Day finished second after starting sixth, according to the finishing order published by SPEED SPORT, TJSlideways and Hoseheads.com, which matches the top five posted by Merced Speedway. Polesitter Colby Copeland was third, Max Mittry fourth and Jett Barnes fifth. Austin Wood, Landon Henry, Dylan Bloomfield, Jeremy Alves and Hayden Klein completed the top 10 in the 18-car field.

The Chase Johnson Classic also included a USAC National Midget feature, won by Buddy Kofoid, and a non-winged Ultimate Sprint Car Series feature, won by Ryan Bernal. Pursley finished third in the USAC National Midget feature, according to USAC.

Sources: Merced Speedway posted results (top five); SPEED SPORT (Nov. 22, 2026, finishing order); TJSlideways (finishing order); Hoseheads.com (posted results); USAC (midget feature). SPEED SPORT’s article body listed a different fourth and fifth than its own results table; the table matches Merced Speedway, TJSlideways and Hoseheads.com and is the order used here.
