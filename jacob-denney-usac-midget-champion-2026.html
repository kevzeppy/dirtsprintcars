Jacob Denney Clinches First USAC National Midget Championship at Bakersfield

Denney finished second in the Jason Leffler Memorial on Nov. 24 to lock up the 2026 title with one race remaining.

Published November 25, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Jacob Denney secured the 2026 USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midget Championship on Tuesday night at Bakersfield Speedway, finishing second behind Daison Pursley in the Jason Leffler Memorial, according to USAC Media. The 21-year-old from Galloway, Ohio, clinched his first national title for Keith Kunz Motorsports with one event left on the schedule.

Denney entered the night with a substantial points lead and needed a solid finish to wrap up the crown. His runner-up result gave him 1,316 points, 83 ahead of Kevin Thomas Jr., with the Turkey Night Grand Prix still to come at Ventura Raceway on Nov. 28. The championship is the 14th for Kunz and the 12th for Curb-Agajanian, and Toyota’s 12th USAC national title since 2013.

Denney led the series with four feature wins in 2026 after dominating the Xtreme Outlaw and POWRi midget ranks in 2025. “I’m just relieved to clinch the championship,” he said after the race. The title makes him the first Ohio driver to win the USAC National Midget crown since Mike Streicher in 1991.
