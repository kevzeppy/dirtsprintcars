Buddy Kofoid Wins World of Outlaws Short Track Nationals at Texarkana

Kofoid passed Cole Macedo on a late restart for his 17th win of the season.

Published October 31, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Michael “Buddy” Kofoid won the 39th World of Outlaws Short Track Nationals on Saturday, Oct. 24, at Texarkana 67 Speedway in Texarkana, Ark. The 40-lap 410 feature paid $25,000 to win.

On a restart with six laps to go, Kofoid ripped the cushion and completed a slide job past Cole Macedo in Turn 1 to take the lead.

The win was Kofoid’s 17th of 2026 in the Roth Motorsports No. 83, breaking the Roth Motorsports single-season World of Outlaws win record of 16 set by Danny Lasoski in 1999.

Top 5: 1. Buddy Kofoid, 2. Cole Macedo, 3. Garet Williamson, 4. Tyler Courtney, 5. David Gravel.

Sources: World of Outlaws
