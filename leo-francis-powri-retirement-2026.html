Leo Francis Retires After 21 Years With POWRi

Francis, half of the father-son team behind Francis Recovery Buggies, had been part of POWRi race-day operations since the series began, POWRi said.

Published November 15, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Leo Francis has retired after 21 years with POWRi Racing, the series announced Saturday, Nov. 14. POWRi said Francis had been a familiar face and an integral part of its race-day operations from the series’ first day.

Francis and his son are the father-son team behind Francis Recovery Buggies, which POWRi called a staple in its history. According to the series, Leo played a key role in building the machines that have served the sport for decades.

In that role, Francis pushed off and towed drivers ranging from future stars getting their start to established names in the sport, POWRi said. The series credited him with treating every driver, team and piece of equipment with care and with doing whatever it took to keep a race night moving. “People like Leo are the backbone of grassroots racing,” the announcement said.

POWRi tagged the announcement to its National Midget, West Midget, WAR Sprint and 410 Outlaw Sprint series. It did not say who will take over his duties.
