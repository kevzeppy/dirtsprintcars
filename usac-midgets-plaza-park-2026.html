USAC National Midgets Resume Golden State Invasion Nov. 18 at Plaza Park

With the Hangtown 100 canceled, the series opens its five-race California finish at Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia, the first USAC National Midgets event at the 1/5-mile track.

Published November 15, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midget Championship resumes its Golden State Invasion on Wednesday, Nov. 18, at Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia, California. This will be the series’ first visit to the 1/5-mile bullring.

Both nights of the Hangtown 100 at Placerville Speedway were canceled because of forecast storms. Officials said ticket refunds are automatic. The remaining schedule is Plaza Park on Nov. 18, Merced Speedway on Nov. 20-21 for the Chase Johnson Classic, Bakersfield Speedway at Kern Raceway on Nov. 24 for the Jason Leffler Memorial, and the 85th Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura Raceway on Nov. 28.

Jacob Denney leads the points by 73 over Kevin Thomas Jr. heading into the stretch. Several drivers, including Jett Yantis, have confirmed full runs of the remaining events.
