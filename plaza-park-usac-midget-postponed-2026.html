Plaza Park USAC Midget Race Postponed for Nov. 18

Saturated grounds and a forecast for more rain led officials to postpone the Wednesday debut of USAC National Midgets at Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia, California.

Published November 17, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The Wednesday, November 18, USAC NOS Energy Drink Midget National Championship race at Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia, California, has been postponed. Track and USAC officials cited saturated grounds and a forecast calling for additional rain leading into race day.

Officials are working on details to potentially reschedule the Good Earth Farms Special Presented by Peelz during this month’s Golden State Invasion. The series continues with races at Merced Speedway on November 20-21, Bakersfield Speedway at Kevin Harvick’s Kern Raceway on November 24, and the Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura Raceway on November 28.

This was to be the first USAC National Midget event at the 1/5-mile Plaza Park oval. Earlier in the month, storms canceled the Hangtown 100 at Placerville Speedway on November 13-14.

Sources: USAC; USAC Racing on X.
