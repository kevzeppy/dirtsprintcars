USAC Postpones National Midget Debut at Plaza Park Raceway

Saturated grounds and a forecast for more rain called off the Wednesday, Nov. 18, Good Earth Farms Special at Visalia, Calif. The Golden State Invasion resumes Nov. 20-21 at Merced Speedway.

Published November 17, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com
Update, Nov. 18: USAC has rescheduled the Plaza Park debut for Monday, Nov. 23. Read the story. The article below was published before the new date was announced.

The Wednesday, Nov. 18, debut of USAC NOS Energy Drink Midget National Championship racing at Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia, California, has been postponed because of saturated grounds and a forecast calling for more rain leading into race day, USAC announced on Nov. 16.

Plaza Park Raceway and USAC officials are working on details to potentially reschedule the Good Earth Farms Special Presented by Peelz at the 1/5-mile dirt oval during this month’s Golden State Invasion, according to USAC. No new date had been announced. USAC said updates will be posted on its website and social media pages and on Plaza Park Raceway’s.

The Plaza Park race was to be the series’ next event after storms canceled the Hangtown 100 at Placerville, according to USAC’s Nov. 11 announcement of that cancellation.

The Golden State Invasion continues Friday and Saturday, Nov. 20-21, at Merced Speedway, followed by Tuesday, Nov. 24, at Bakersfield Speedway at Kevin Harvick’s Kern Raceway and Saturday, Nov. 28, at Ventura Raceway, USAC said.

Sources: Plaza Park USAC Midget Race on Nov 18 Postponed, Richie Murray, USAC Media (Nov. 16, 2026); USAC press release emailed Nov. 16, 2026; Storms Cancel Placerville’s Hangtown 100, USAC Midgets Resume Nov 18 at Plaza Park, USAC (Nov. 11, 2026). Photo: Rich Forman / USAC.
