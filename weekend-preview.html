Weekend preview

This week: World of Outlaws in Texarkana, USAC in Arizona

Seven days of sprint car racing at two venues, in Arkansas and Arizona.

Published October 20, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The calendar for October 20 through 27, as published by the series and tracks. Check each track for start times, ticketing and weather before you go.

Friday, October 23, Texarkana 67 Speedway, Texarkana, Arkansas. The World of Outlaws open their Texarkana weekend with the Jason Johnson Classic.

Saturday, October 24, Texarkana 67 Speedway. The World of Outlaws run the Short Track Nationals.

Friday and Saturday, October 23 and 24, Central Arizona Raceway, Casa Grande, Arizona. The USAC AMSOIL National Sprint Cars race the 59th Western World Championships, alongside the USAC CRA Sprint Cars and the USAC Southwest Sprint Cars. On Friday the pits open at 2 p.m. Mountain time, gates at 4 p.m. and hot laps at 6 p.m.

No midget events are confirmed for this window yet. We will add them as schedules are verified.

Looking ahead: the World of Outlaws World Finals start November 4 at The Dirt Track at Charlotte in Concord, North Carolina.

Sources: USAC event info, World of Outlaws.
