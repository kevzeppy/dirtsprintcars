Nov 2: Gravel Eyes Historic Three-Peat at Charlotte World Finals

David Gravel and Big Game Motorsports hold a 124-point lead over Michael Kofoid heading into the Dave Smith Motors World of Outlaws World Finals (Nov. 4-7) at The Dirt Track at Charlotte. Only Kinser, Schatz and Sweet have ever three-peated. Gravel’s just hoping the Queen City doesn’t crown someone else this week.
