2027 World of Outlaws Sprint Car Pennsylvania Dates Finalized

The PA Posse rivalry returns for eight rounds in 2027, beginning with the Gettysburg Clash at Lincoln Speedway on May 12.

Published November 13, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series has finalized eight Pennsylvania dates for the 2027 schedule, setting the stage for another season of the PA Posse rivalry.

The 2026 scoreboard showed five wins for the World of Outlaws and three for the PA Posse. The series owns bragging rights heading into 2027.

The rivalry rekindles Wednesday, May 12, at Lincoln Speedway’s Gettysburg Clash. Michael “Buddy” Kofoid and Roth Motorsports have won the last two runnings.

Williams Grove Speedway hosts the HVAC Distributors Morgan Cup on Friday-Saturday, May 14-15. Kasey Kahne won the opener this year; David Gravel took the finale and the Cup.

Summer dates include the Bricker’s Bash at BAPS Motor Speedway on Thursday, July 29. Chase Dietz won for the Posse at BAPS in 2026. The C&D Rigging Summer Nationals follow at Williams Grove on July 30-31. Kofoid and Gravel swept the Summer Nationals for the World of Outlaws this year.

The final Pennsylvania showdown is the 65th J&S Classics National Open at Williams Grove on Friday-Saturday, Oct. 1-2. Gravel won the opener in 2026; Dietz took the title and $75,000.

Further ticketing details will be announced on the World of Outlaws website and social channels. Every race streams live on DIRTVision.

Source: World of Outlaws (November 12, 2026).
