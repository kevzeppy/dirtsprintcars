Emerson Axsom Follows Chili Bowl Win With World of Outlaws Rookie of the Year Title

The Franklin, Ind., driver won the Golden Driller in January, took his first World of Outlaws feature in September and closed his first full-time season as the Kevin Gobrecht Rookie of the Year.

Published November 10, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Emerson Axsom finished his first full-time World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series season as the Kevin Gobrecht Rookie of the Year presented by Five Star Bodies, an award announced at the series’ awards celebration on Nov. 8 in Charlotte, N.C. (our banquet report). The year began with a win in the Chili Bowl Nationals, a crown jewel, and included his first series feature victory. World of Outlaws statistics credit Axsom and the Klaasmeyer/Petry Motorsports No. 27 with one win, 11 top-five finishes, 32 top 10s and 66 laps led, and the series’ points table lists him ninth with 8,946 points.

Axsom won the 40th Chili Bowl Nationals on Jan. 17 in Tulsa, Okla., driving the No. 39A for Kevin Swindell’s team. Swindell, a four-time Chili Bowl winner as a driver, had messaged him on Instagram the previous summer to ask if he wanted the ride, NASCAR.com reported. Starting second in the 55-lap A-feature, Axsom passed pole-sitter Kyle Larson, the 2025 winner, on the fourth lap. On lap 16 Larson flipped after contact with the slowed car of C.J. Leary and was eliminated, and Axsom held the lead through challenges from Logan Seavey and Justin Grant. A late caution for Grant’s flip in turn three set up a green-white-checkered finish, and Axsom held off Kevin Thomas Jr., who had started 18th, to win. Hank Davis was third, Seavey fourth and Briggs Danner fifth, according to the official results.

The win followed several seasons of moving between divisions. According to the World of Outlaws, Axsom ran the USAC National Midget Series in 2021 and USAC National Sprint Cars in 2022 and 2023, then committed fully to winged sprint cars in 2024 with Klaasmeyer/Petry Motorsports, winning that year at Lincoln Park Speedway and Knoxville Raceway. He was the Knoxville Nationals Rookie of the Year in 2024 and won his first All Star Circuit of Champions feature at Eldora Speedway in 2025. In July 2025 he filled in for two months in the KCP Racing No. 18 after Giovanni Scelzi and the team separated, then returned to the Klaasmeyer/Petry car. On Feb. 3, 2026, the series announced he would run the full World of Outlaws schedule in the No. 27 for car owners Dale Klaasmeyer and Scott Petry. His father, Joe Axsom, serves as crew chief.

The season started slowly. Axsom had no top 10s in his first six races, then finished second at Tri-State Speedway in Haubstadt, Ind., on April 19, his best result of the year at that point. That finish moved him 28 points ahead of Ashton Torgerson in the rookie standings, the World of Outlaws reported. His first win came Sept. 6 at Huset’s Speedway in Brandon, S.D., in the L.G. Everist Huset’s Shootout finale. Starting second in the 40-lap feature, he traded the lead with Carson Macedo and held off David Gravel and Michael Kofoid on late restarts for a $25,000 payday. The series listed him as its 160th different feature winner, the fifth first-time winner of 2026 and the 21st different winner of the season.

Axsom was strong through the second half. Before the World Finals, the World of Outlaws noted he had 19 top 10s in his previous 23 races. At The Dirt Track at Charlotte he won Thursday’s C-main to qualify for the feature, started 25th and finished 22nd. He was 12th on Friday and 18th on Saturday, qualifying for all three A-mains.

Under the Five Star Bodies program announced by the World of Outlaws in February, the sprint car Rookie of the Year receives a new body and a $10,000 bonus. The 2027 World of Outlaws season opens Feb. 10-13 at Volusia Speedway Park in Barberville, Fla.
