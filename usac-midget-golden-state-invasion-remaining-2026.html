USAC National Midgets Resume Nov. 18 at Plaza Park After Hangtown Cancellation

Five Golden State Invasion events remain after storms forced the cancellation of the Hangtown 100 at Placerville.

Published November 12, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midgets continue their Golden State Invasion of California with a stop at Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia on Wednesday, Nov. 18. The series then visits Merced Speedway for two nights on Nov. 20-21, Bakersfield Speedway at Kevin Harvick’s Kern Raceway for the Jason Leffler Memorial on Nov. 24, and closes the season with the Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura Raceway on Nov. 28.

Ten different drivers have won USAC National Midget features so far in 2026. Jacob Denney leads the standings with the final seven races originally scheduled; two of those were the canceled Hangtown nights. Daison Pursley is entered for the remaining California races in the CB Industries No. 86.

Refunds for the canceled Hangtown 100 tickets are automatic. No makeup date was set for Placerville.
