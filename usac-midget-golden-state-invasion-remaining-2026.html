Jacob Denney Leads USAC National Midgets Into Five-Race California Finish

The Hangtown 100 cancellation leaves five races, starting Nov. 18 at Plaza Park Raceway. Denney leads Kevin Thomas Jr. by 73 points.

Published November 12, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midgets have five races left in the 2026 season after officials canceled both nights of the Hangtown 100, Nov. 13-14 at Placerville Speedway, because of a forecast for heavy rain, USAC announced Nov. 11. The Golden State Invasion now opens Wednesday, Nov. 18, at Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia, California.

The series then runs two nights at Merced Speedway, Nov. 20-21, for the Chase Johnson Classic, and the Jason Leffler Memorial on Tuesday, Nov. 24, at Bakersfield Speedway at Kevin Harvick’s Kern Raceway. The season ends Saturday, Nov. 28, with the Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura Raceway.

Jacob Denney leads the driver standings with 1,130 points, 73 ahead of Kevin Thomas Jr. (1,057) and 124 ahead of Justin Grant (1,006), according to USAC’s standings, which were last updated after the Sept. 26 race at Eldora Speedway. Gavin Miller (970), Jakeb Boxell (968) and Kale Drake (941) round out the top six. Denney drives the Keith Kunz/Curb-Agajanian Motorsports No. 67, which leads the entrant standings.

Ten drivers have won the 17 National Midget features completed this season, according to USAC’s schedule. Denney has four wins, Grant and Thomas have three each, and Hayden Reinbold, Karter Sarff, Logan Seavey, Briggs Danner, Miller, Drake and Boxell have one each.

Hangtown 100 tickets will be refunded automatically, less credit card processing fees, and no makeup date was announced. Read the Hangtown 100 cancellation story.
