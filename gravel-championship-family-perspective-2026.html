Gravel Reflects on Third Straight WoO Title with Growing Family

David Gravel talks about sharing his third consecutive World of Outlaws Sprint Car championship with son Levi and daughter Holly.

Published November 20, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

David Gravel and his son Levi walked side by side on the dirt at The Dirt Track at Charlotte wearing matching blue champion hats after the 2026 World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series season concluded, according to a World of Outlaws feature published November 20, 2026. Confetti covered the front straightaway as Gravel secured his third straight title with Big Game Motorsports.

The 34-year-old’s championship run has coincided with his family growing. He and wife Jillian welcomed son Levi in late 2022. Daughter Holly was born last December following his second straight title. “It makes it more special,” Gravel said of the family’s growth during the title streak. “Levi’s at the age now where he knows every driver and knows what’s going on. The family came on the road with me for a month this year. Life changes every couple years, and you get different perspectives. Our family is growing, and it’s definitely cool to share these memories with them.”

Gravel finished the season with 11 wins, 32 podiums, 48 top fives, and 66 top 10s in 75 starts. His average finish was 5.37. The team’s highlight included a Series-record $300,000 payday at the BillionAuto.com Huset’s High Bank Nationals in June.
