David Gravel Honored at 2026 World of Outlaws Awards Banquet

David Gravel was crowned the 2026 World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series champion at the awards banquet in Charlotte, securing his third consecutive title with Big Game Motorsports.

Published November 21, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series teams gathered in Charlotte, N.C., to celebrate the 2026 season at the Championship Awards Banquet. Top drivers were honored and special awards handed out before the ceremony crowned David Gravel the 2026 series champion.

Gravel claimed his third consecutive championship with Big Game Motorsports, joining an elite group. Only Steve Kinser, Donny Schatz, Brad Sweet, and now Gravel have claimed three straight titles through 49 seasons of World of Outlaws Sprint Car action. His third title overall ties him with Sammy Swindell for fourth-most in series history.

Gravel tallied 11 wins, 32 podiums, 48 top fives, and 66 top 10s in 75 starts. His biggest highlight came at Huset’s Speedway in June with a series-record $300,000 payday for the Huset’s High Bank Nationals. Point fund payouts for the top five included $350,000 for Gravel, $280,000 for Michael “Buddy” Kofoid, $227,500 for Sheldon Haudenschild, $201,250 for Carson Macedo, and $175,000 for Logan Schuchart.

Source: The Left Turn | West Bend News (Ron Burt, Nov. 21, 2026); also World of Outlaws (Nov. 10, 2026).
