Aiden Stull Kicks Off 2026 World of Outlaws iRacing Pro Series with Volusia Win

Aiden Stull led early and held off Alex Bergeron to win the opening feature of the 2026 World of Outlaws Pro Series Sprint Car Championship on iRacing at Volusia Speedway Park on Monday night, November 16.

Stull took the lead from Tanner Pettit and stayed in front. Bergeron, who set quick time, worked from eighth after the invert to second before a spin dropped him to third. Pettit finished second. Stull leads the championship by one point over Bergeron heading to Huset’s Speedway next Monday.

The series runs five races through mid-December, with $25,000 total prize money split between the Sprint Car and Late Model championships. Sources: World of Outlaws.
