Giovanni Scelzi Serves Up Hot Sauce at Friday World Finals

Giovanni Scelzi led wire-to-wire after ripping past polesitter Sheldon Haudenschild on the opening lap to win Friday's 25-lap World of Outlaws feature at Charlotte. Tanner Holmes chased him home in second.

Published November 7, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Charlotte got a fresh dose of Hot Sauce on Friday night. Giovanni Scelzi, in the Spire Motorsports Chili's No. 77, started on the front row and immediately made the move for the lead on the opening circuit, then never looked back in the 25-lap World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink feature at The Dirt Track at Charlotte.

Tanner Holmes charged into second late and closed the gap in traffic, but ran out of laps as Scelzi took the checkered flag by 0.384 seconds. Sheldon Haudenschild, the polesitter, finished third. Cameron Smith and Buddy Schweibinz completed the top five. David Gravel, already the 2026 champion, was ninth.

The win makes Scelzi the 24th different World of Outlaws winner in 2026, one shy of the record for most in a season with one night remaining. It's his 11th career WoO victory. Scelzi noted the cushion got lipped up after the till, but credited the track crew and fans who stuck around for nearly 12 hours. Winning in Spire's home state with the Chili's car was a nice bonus — seven years since his last Charlotte triumph.

The World Finals wrap Saturday with the $25,000-to-win season finale. Hot Sauce is back on the menu.
