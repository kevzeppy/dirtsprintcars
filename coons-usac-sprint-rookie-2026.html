Nov 3: Cale Coons Named USAC National Sprint Car Top Rookie for 2026

Cale Coons, son of Hall of Famer Jerry Coons Jr., took 2026 USAC AMSOIL National Sprint Car Max Papis Innovations Rookie of the Year after a season that included his first feature win and 11th in points.

Published November 3, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Chip off the old block. The Greencastle, Indiana driver led 32 laps, scored four top-fives, and racked up 16 heat wins in the family Dooling Autosports entry. First son of a past National Sprint Car champ to claim rookie honors. Not bad for a guy whose dad already filled the trophy case — now the next generation is adding to the pile without asking permission.
