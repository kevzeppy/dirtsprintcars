D.J. Netto Wins 360 Winged Feature at Central Arizona Raceway

Netto started on the pole and won the 25-lap feature Saturday, Nov. 21, at Casa Grande, Ariz. Cale Thomas was second and Wyatt Miller third. Netto also won Friday’s winged feature.

Published November 22, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

D.J. Netto of Hanford, California, won the 25-lap 360 winged sprint car feature Saturday, Nov. 21, at Central Arizona Raceway in Casa Grande, according to the track’s official event results. Netto drove the No. 88N.

SPEED SPORT and results posted by Hoseheads.com show Netto started on the pole and won ahead of Cale Thomas, who started third. Wyatt Miller started second and finished third. Elijah Gile, who won the companion USAC Southwest non-wing feature, finished fourth after starting fifth, and Kade Higday was fifth. Braden Chiaramonte, Aydan Saunders, Caleb Saiz, Jaxon Porter and Tuesday Scearce completed the top 10 in the 18-car field.

Netto also won Friday’s 360 winged feature at the Dennis Matousek Memorial, according to Central Arizona Raceway’s Night 1 event results. Saturday’s companion USAC Avanti Windows & Doors Southwest Sprint Car feature was won by Elijah Gile, with R.J. Johnson finishing second to clinch his sixth series title, according to USAC.

Sources: Central Arizona Raceway Saturday event results (Netto, No. 88N, Hanford, Calif.); SPEED SPORT (Nov. 22, 2026, finishing order and starting positions); Hoseheads.com (posted results); Central Arizona Raceway Friday event results (Netto Night 1 win); USAC (Southwest feature and title).
