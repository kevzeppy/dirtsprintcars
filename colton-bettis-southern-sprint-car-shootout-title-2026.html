Colton Bettis Wins Southern Sprint Car Shootout Series Finale and Third Straight Title

The 17-year-old started fifth, led before halfway and beat Daniel Miller by 4.140 seconds at Inverness, Fla., on Saturday, Nov. 14.

Published November 16, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Colton Bettis won the 40-lap BG Products Southern Sprint Car Shootout Series feature at Citrus County Speedway in Inverness, Florida, on Saturday, Nov. 14, the series’ season finale, and clinched his third consecutive series championship, SPEED SPORT reported. Bettis started fifth, took the lead before the halfway mark and won by 4.140 seconds over Daniel Miller. It was his sixth series win of the season.

Larry Brazil Jr. finished third, Brian Gingras fourth and Jim Childers fifth. Bruce Brantley, Bill Gise, Jerry Paquin, Ryan Adema and LJ Grimm completed the top 10. Steven Hollinger was listed as a non-starter, according to the published finish.

Bettis needed only to start the feature to secure the title, according to SPEED SPORT. The 17-year-old driver of the No. 61 now has more series championships than any other driver in the history of the series, which began in 2016. SPEED SPORT also reported that Bettis arrived by helicopter at an airport next to the track.

The Southern Sprint Car Shootout Series races winged sprint cars, and its Citrus County Speedway events use a 40-lap feature format, according to the series website.

Sources: Bettis Delivers At Citrus County, David Sink, SPEED SPORT (Nov. 15, 2026); Hoseheads.com results (Nov. 14, 2026); Event information, BG Products Southern Sprint Car Shootout Series.
