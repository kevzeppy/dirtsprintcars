Lachlan McHugh Wins Australian Sprintcar Title Prelude at Toowoomba

Lachlan McHugh took victory in a crash-riddled Prelude to the Title feature race at Hi-Tec Oils Toowoomba Speedway on November 19, 2026, ahead of the 64th Nutrend Homes Australian Sprintcar Championship.

The 2024 Australian champion in the NQ7 car won by 1.12 seconds over Ryan Newton, who charged from 11th. Brock Hallett finished third, James McFadden fourth and Brendan Quinn fifth.

It was McHugh’s 40th feature win at Toowoomba. The race featured multiple incidents, including a late rollover by Randy Morgan.

Source: Speedcafe.com report, November 19, 2026.
