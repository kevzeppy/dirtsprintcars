Lachlan McHugh Wins Australian Sprintcar Title Prelude at Toowoomba

McHugh won the 35-lap Prelude to the Title at Hi-Tec Oils Toowoomba Speedway by 1.12 seconds over Ryan Newton, who started 11th. Brock Hallett finished third. The 64th Australian Sprintcar Championship follows Friday and Saturday at Toowoomba.

Published November 19, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Lachlan McHugh won the Prelude to the Title on Wednesday, Nov. 18, at Hi-Tec Oils Toowoomba Speedway in Toowoomba, Queensland, according to Speedcafe. The 2024 Australian champion, driving the NQ7, beat Ryan Newton (Q66) by 1.12 seconds after Newton charged from 11th on the grid. Brock Hallett (Q5) finished third, James McFadden (WX23) recovered from a lap 1 incident to finish fourth, and Brendan Quinn (S37) was fifth. Speedcafe reported it was McHugh’s 40th feature win at Toowoomba.

Marcus Dumesny led early in the 35-lap feature, with McHugh running second. A yellow came out after one lap when Dylan Menz slid down from the top in turn 4 and made contact with Alex Attard, collecting McFadden, Mick Saller and Nic Whell. McFadden was push-started and continued, while Saller and Whell were out. McHugh caught Dumesny with eight laps to go, and with three laps remaining Dumesny tagged the outside wall exiting turn 2, then hit the wall heavily at turn 3 and was out of the race, according to the report.

McHugh led the restart, and a third stoppage came on the final lap when Menz hit the wall in turn 1. McHugh pulled away from Newton on the final restart. As the checkered flag fell, B Main winner Randy Morgan slid into the turn 4 wall and rolled against the catch fence; he was classified 10th. Max Guilford finished sixth from the front row, followed by Jy Corbet, Luke Oldfield and Aaron Kelly.

The Prelude was a standalone race meeting held in place of an official practice night. The 64th Nutrend Homes Australian Sprintcar Championship runs Friday and Saturday, Nov. 20–21, at Toowoomba, the first time the venue has hosted the title.

Source: McHugh wins chaotic Australian Sprintcar title prelude, Jack Fletcher, Speedcafe (Nov. 19, 2026).
