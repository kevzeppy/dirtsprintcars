Carson Macedo Wins Saturday World Finals Finale at Charlotte

Carson Macedo started eighth and won Saturday’s 25-lap NOS Energy Drink feature at the Dave Smith Motors World of Outlaws World Finals at The Dirt Track at Charlotte. Michael Kofoid finished second and Giovanni Scelzi third.

Published November 8, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Carson Macedo won Saturday’s 25-lap World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series season finale at The Dirt Track at Charlotte, charging from eighth in the Jason Johnson Racing No. 41 to a $25,000 victory. Macedo led the final nine laps and beat Michael “Buddy” Kofoid by 0.324 seconds, according to official World of Outlaws results. Giovanni Scelzi, who started on the pole after winning the Toyota Dash and had won Friday’s feature, led the first 16 laps and finished third. Buddy Schweibinz and Steven Snyder Jr. rounded out the top five.

Charlotte Motor Speedway reported an opening-lap caution after contact in Turn 2. Macedo reached second by the ninth lap, then took the lead with a slide job on Scelzi on lap 17. Charlotte Motor Speedway said that pass came underneath Scelzi in Turn 2 with eight laps remaining. Kofoid moved into second late and tried a slide job in Turns 3 and 4 that Macedo drove around. “When I got to second, it was everything I had every single lap as hard as I could go as thin as I could get it to make speed and not get over it,” Macedo said in the World of Outlaws recap. “Buddy was coming there at the end. … The one slider I drove around, I think it would’ve been inches from disaster, but luckily we made it around there.”

Tyler Courtney started second and was a DNF on lap 7. Thursday winner Rico Abreu and Cole Macedo were DNFs on lap 0. Chase Randall drove from 23rd to 14th to win the KSE Racing Products Hard Charger award, gaining nine positions for his second hard-charger honor of the week. The 28-car feature was completed in 12 minutes, 46 seconds. Tanner Holmes finished sixth, followed by Brock Zearfoss, Sheldon Haudenschild, Logan Schuchart and Bill Balog. Earlier, heat races went to Scelzi, Courtney, Macedo and Kofoid. Scelzi also won the SPA Technique No. 1 redraw, and David Gravel won the Micro-Lite Last Chance Showdown before finishing 17th in the feature from 21st. Charlotte Motor Speedway reported that Gravel battled engine issues Saturday.

World of Outlaws said the win was Macedo’s eighth of the 2026 season and the 66th of his series career, tying him with Bobby Davis Jr. and Craig Dollansky for 14th all-time. It was also the 90th World of Outlaws victory for Jason Johnson Racing, 10 wins from becoming the seventh organization to reach 100. Charlotte Motor Speedway said it was Macedo’s second career win at The Dirt Track at Charlotte. Kofoid’s runner-up finish in the Roth Motorsports No. 83 finalized a series-best 35 podiums this year. Scelzi, in the Spire Motorsports No. 77, said he wore his equipment out after leading the first 16 laps and finished two spots short of back-to-back Charlotte wins.

“To look at an eight-win season and be disappointed shows we have high expectations, and we want to do our best to be the best team on the road,” Macedo said. “It’s not from lack of effort. Philip (Dietz), Adam (Zimmerman), and Robby (McQuinn) are putting in the hours and putting in the effort.” Gravel had already clinched his third consecutive World of Outlaws championship Thursday. After Saturday’s finale he was formally crowned at The Dirt Track at Charlotte alongside World of Outlaws Late Model Series champion Bobby Pierce and Super DIRTcar Series champion Alex Yankowski, who World of Outlaws said became the youngest champion in that series’ history. Gravel ended the year with 11 wins, 48 top-five finishes and 66 top-10s in 75 starts, and an 86-point lead over Kofoid. The 2027 World of Outlaws season opens at Volusia Speedway Park’s Federated Auto Parts DIRTcar Nationals on Feb. 10-13.

Sources: World of Outlaws (official Saturday recap, Nov. 7, 2026) and official feature results (Nov. 7, 2026); World of Outlaws championship recap (Nov. 8, 2026); Charlotte Motor Speedway (Nov. 7, 2026).
