Justin Peck Wins Trophy Cup Night 1 at Thunderbowl

Peck went from 4th to the lead on lap one and never looked back.

Published October 30, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Justin Peck won the 30-lap 410 winged sprint car feature on Night 1 of Trophy Cup 32 on Thursday, Oct. 29, at Thunderbowl Raceway in Tulare, Calif. Peck started 4th in the Rudeen Racing No. 26, took the lead on lap one and led the rest of the way.

Jace Park closed in at the halfway point in lapped traffic, but once Peck cleared it, he was gone.

Top 5: 1. Justin Peck, 2. Jace Park, 3. Jake Andreotti, 4. Kaleb Montgomery, 5. Gauge Garcia.

Nights 2 and 3 are still to come on Friday, Oct. 30, and Saturday, Oct. 31.
