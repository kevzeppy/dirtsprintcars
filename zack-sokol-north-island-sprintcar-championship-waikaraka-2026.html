Zack Sokol Wins North Island Sprintcar Championship at Waikaraka, Jonathan Allard Second

Sokol won his heat and the 30-lap championship feature at Lucas Oil Waikaraka Family Speedway in Auckland on Saturday, Nov. 21. American Jonathan Allard was second and Cole Wood third.

Published November 27, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Zack Sokol won the North Island Sprintcar Championship on Saturday, Nov. 21, at Lucas Oil Waikaraka Family Speedway in Onehunga, Auckland, New Zealand, according to results published by DriverDB and Sprint Car Ratings. Sokol finished the 30-lap feature 3.185 seconds ahead of American Jonathan Allard, according to DriverDB’s timing results, and the two sources list the same finishing order. Cole Wood was third, Stephen Taylor fourth and Rodney Wood fifth.

Sokol also won the first of three heats, beating Cole Wood by 2.143 seconds over 12 laps, and set the fastest lap of the feature at 13.866 seconds, DriverDB’s results show. Keaton Dahm finished seventh, Luke Brown eighth and Jayden Worthington ninth, one lap down. Michael Pickens, Ryan O’Connor and Brad Mosen were all out after one lap, and Hayden Williams did not start the feature, according to DriverDB. Waikaraka’s published schedule listed the North Island championship for Nov. 21, with midgets, TQs, F2s and quarter midgets also on the card.

Allard had won two sprint car features at Baypark this season before the trip to Auckland. The New Zealand season continues Saturday, Nov. 28, with sprint cars at Baypark, where defending New Zealand champion Daniel Thomas makes his season debut, and at Ruapuna Speedway in Christchurch. Both meetings run Friday night, Nov. 27, U.S. Central time. We found no news report of the race, so this story relies on the published results.

Sources: DriverDB, 2026 Midas NZ North Island Sprintcar Championships feature results (finishing order, margin, fastest lap) and Heat 1 results; Sprint Car Ratings, Waikaraka North Island Sprintcar Championship results (finishing order); Waikaraka Speedway race schedule (date and card).
