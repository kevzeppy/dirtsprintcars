Kaylee Bryson Out at Abacus; Team Says It Will Be Back in 2027

Bryson said she was told Monday she would not continue with Abacus Racing. The team thanked Logan Seavey for four years and said it will be back in 2027, with details at PRI.

Published November 9, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Kaylee Bryson will not return to the Abacus Racing Silver Crown program in 2027.

Bryson said on social media Monday that she received a call that morning informing her she would not be continuing with the team. She said her 2027 plans are not finalized and that she is looking for new opportunities.

Later Monday, Abacus Racing released a statement titled “Response to Recent Events.” The team thanked Logan Seavey for four years and listed the results from their partnership: five USAC Silver Crown wins and the 2023 championship, 26 USAC National Sprint Car wins and the 2024 championship, and 12 USAC National Midget wins and the 2023 championship.

On the team’s future, Abacus Racing wrote: “We will be back in 2027.” Details of the new program, the driver, and other team news will be announced at PRI.

The statement follows Seavey’s announcement last week that he was leaving Abacus Racing after four years, effective immediately. Bryson also said earlier this week that Abacus is not bringing any cars to the USAC National Midget West Coast swing or the Chili Bowl Nationals.

Sources: Kaylee Bryson social media (Nov. 9, 2026); Abacus Racing statement (Nov. 9, 2026); Logan Seavey social media (Nov. 3, 2026). Related prior coverage: Logan Seavey and Abacus Racing Part Ways.
