Weekly preview

This week: USAC National Midgets open the Golden State Invasion at the Hangtown 100

The USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midgets begin their seven-race California stretch at Placerville, BAPS hosts a $10,000-to-win 410 show, and Australia and New Zealand continue their seasons.

Published November 8, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The schedule for Sunday, November 8 through Saturday, November 14, as published by the series and tracks. Check each track for start times, ticketing and weather before you go.

Wing sprint cars

The World of Outlaws, High Limit Racing, All Star Circuit of Champions and American Sprint Car Series seasons are complete after last week’s finales.

Saturday, November 14, BAPS Motor Speedway, York Haven, Pennsylvania. The Heavy Equipment & Loaders Sprint Showdown for 410 sprint cars is listed at $10,000 to win. The program is scheduled for 3 p.m. ET and streams on PA Posse. Source: BAPS Motor Speedway / MyRacePass, PA Posse.

Friday and Saturday, November 13–14, Placerville Speedway, Placerville, California. Winged 360 sprint cars share the Hangtown 100 card with the USAC National Midgets, the first time 360s have been the companion class. Features both days are listed at $2,000 to win. Front gates open at 1 p.m. PT and cars go on track at 2 p.m. each day. Source: Hangtown 100, USAC, FloRacing.

Friday and Saturday, November 13–14, Vado Speedway Park, Vado, New Mexico. The track lists its Gaerte Engines 360 Winged Sprint Series season finale over two nights. Friday: pit gates 4 p.m., front gates 5 p.m., racing at 7 p.m. Saturday: pit gates 1 p.m., front gates 3 p.m., racing at 5 p.m. Source: Vado Speedway Park.

Non-wing sprint cars

Saturday, November 14, Central Arizona Raceway, Casa Grande, Arizona. USAC’s national calendar lists the USAC Southwest Sprint Car Series (360 non-wing) at Central Arizona Raceway. R.J. Johnson leads the standings with 1,039 points after October 24 at Western World. Dayton Shelton is second with 831, 208 points back. Koen Crawford is third with 811 and Daylin Perreira fourth with 808. USAC also lists follow-up dates at the same track on November 20 and 21. Source: USAC.

The USAC AMSOIL National Sprint Car, USAC Silver Crown and USAC CRA seasons are complete.

Midgets and USAC

Friday and Saturday, November 13–14, Placerville Speedway, Placerville, California. The Hangtown 100 opens the Golden State Invasion, the seven-race California close to the 2026 USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midget championship. It is the only 100-lap USAC National Midget feature of the year. Friday’s 30-lap feature pays $5,000 to win. Saturday’s 100-lap feature pays $10,000 to win, with an open red flag between laps 40 and 60. Both features award 70 points to the winner. Front gates open at 1 p.m. PT and cars go on track at 2 p.m. both days; promoters moved the start earlier because of typical November temperatures. The event streams on FloRacing.

Jacob Denney leads the championship with 1,130 points after the September 26 4-Crown Nationals at Eldora Speedway. Kevin Thomas Jr. is second with 1,057, 73 points back. Justin Grant is third with 1,006, Gavin Miller fourth with 970 and Jakeb Boxell fifth with 968.

Kyle Larson is entered in both the USAC National Midgets and the winged 360 sprint cars. Larson, from Elk Grove, California, won the USAC Midget portion of the Hangtown 100 in 2019 and 2023. He made his first sprint car start at Placerville in 2007 and earned his first sprint car win there later that season. He has been a co-promoter of the event with Russell Motorsports since 2025. Source: FloRacing, Hangtown 100, USAC.

Saturday’s heat lineups are seeded from Friday’s finishing order, with Friday’s winner as the No. 1 seed. The top six transferring seeds who advance through Saturday’s heats invert to start the 100-lap feature.

Friday and Saturday, November 13–14, Adobe Mountain Speedway, Glendale, Arizona. The 5th Annual Tribute to Billy Shuman features the POWRi Southwest Ecotec Midget Series, with A-Class non-wing, restricted and junior sprints also listed. Source: dirtfan.com / Adobe Mountain Speedway listings, TrackRabbit, AutoLNK.

Looking ahead: Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia on November 18 (the series’ first visit there), the Chase Johnson Classic at Merced Speedway on November 20–21, the 12th Jason Leffler Memorial on November 24 at Bakersfield Speedway at Kevin Harvick’s Kern Raceway, and the 85th Turkey Night Grand Prix on November 28 at Ventura Raceway.

Australia and New Zealand

Saturday, November 14, Perth Motorplex, Kwinana Beach, Western Australia. The 45th annual Scardifields Smash Repairs King of Wings is Round 4 of the Maddington Toyota Sprintcar Series. Round 3 was the WA Sprintcar Title at Bunbury on November 7. The card also includes 360 sprint cars, limited sprint cars and Formula 500s. Competitor entry is listed at 2:30 p.m. local time, main spectator gates at 4 p.m., and the feature program about 5:30 to 10 p.m. Source: Perth Motorplex, Speedway Illustrated News MTS calendar.

Saturday, November 14, Latrobe Speedway, Latrobe, Tasmania. The CJD Equipment Season Opener includes sprint cars among the divisions. Public access is listed at 2 p.m. local time and main-track racing at 6 p.m. Source: Latrobe Speedway.

Saturday, November 14, Ruapuna Speedway, Christchurch, New Zealand. The Daltons Canterbury Championships. Source: Sprintcars United.

Saturday, November 14, Auckland City Speedway (Waikaraka), Auckland, New Zealand. Opening night. Source: Sprintcars United.

Looking ahead: the Australian Sprintcar Championship runs November 18–21 at Toowoomba Speedway in Queensland. Wednesday, November 18 is the prelude, Thursday, November 19 is the calcutta, Friday and Saturday, November 20–21 are championship nights 1 and 2, and Sunday, November 22 is the rain date. Source: Sprintcar World / Toowoomba Speedway.
