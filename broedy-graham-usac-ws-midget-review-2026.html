Broedy Graham Wins 2026 USAC Western States Midget Championship

Graham finished fourth in the Ventura finale and won his first series title by 31 points after Drake Cardey spun with two laps left. He also won the Billy’s Tequila Cup.

Published November 12, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Broedy Graham of Bakersfield, California, is the 2026 USAC Avanti Windows & Doors Western States Midget champion. The 18-year-old clinched the title with a fourth-place finish in the Nov. 7 season finale at Ventura Raceway, and his BGR Racing No. 66 team won the entrant title.

Graham was running sixth with two laps to go while points leader Drake Cardey ran second. Cardey was caught in a four-car tangle at the bottom of Turn 2 and spun to a stop in the infield. He finished 15th, and Graham won the championship 863-832. Graham had taken the points lead from Cardey with one race left and held a six-point edge through an eight-week break before the finale.

Graham led the series with 10 top-five finishes, four fast qualifying times and five heat race wins in 12 starts, and he tied Cardey for the most top-10 finishes with 11. His first two series wins came in back-to-back weeks, in June at Merced Speedway and on July 4 at Santa Maria Speedway. He is the third Bakersfield driver in four seasons to win the title, after Brody Fuson in 2023 and Cade Lewis in 2024.

Graham also won the Billy’s Tequila Cup, a points title for the three Western States Midget races at Ventura, by one point over Jett Yantis. Cardey, who won at Imperial Valley in March and Santa Maria in June for Tom Dunkel Racing, was named Rookie of the Year. Yantis led the series with five wins, and C.J. Sarna was named Most Improved Driver after finishing third in points.

Graham raced the season while playing defensive end as a senior on the Liberty High School football team. His younger brother, Blayden Graham, plans to join him in a two-car USAC Western States Midget effort in 2027.

Source: Golden Graham! 2026 USAC Western States Midget Review, Richie Murray – USAC Media (Nov. 10, 2026); SPEED SPORT (Nov. 8, 2026).
