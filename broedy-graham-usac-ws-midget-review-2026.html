Broedy Graham Wins 2026 USAC Western States Midget Championship

Graham finished fourth in the Ventura finale to claim his first series title by 31 points after Drake Cardey spun with two laps remaining. He also won the Billy’s Tequila Cup.

Published November 11, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Broedy Graham of Bakersfield, California, is the 2026 USAC Avanti Windows & Doors Western States Midget champion. The 18-year-old secured the title with a fourth-place finish in the Nov. 7 season finale at Ventura Raceway.

With two laps to go, Graham was running sixth while Cardey, who led the points chase, ran second. A multi-car incident on the bottom of turn two sent Cardey spinning to a 15th-place finish. Graham held on for fourth and a 31-point championship margin (863-832).

Graham led the series with 10 top-five finishes, 11 top-10s, four fast qualifying times and five heat wins in 12 starts. He scored back-to-back feature wins in June at Merced and Santa Maria. He is the third Bakersfield driver in four years to win the title, following Brody Fuson (2023) and Cade Lewis (2024).

Graham also claimed the Billy’s Tequila Cup for the three Ventura events, edging Jett Yantis by one point. Drake Cardey was named Rookie of the Year. Jett Yantis led the series with five wins.

Graham balanced high school football as a senior defensive end for the Liberty Patriots with his racing schedule. His younger brother Blayden is expected to join the two-car BGR Racing effort in 2027.

Source: SC Motorsports Media / Richie Murray – USAC Media (Nov. 10, 2026).
