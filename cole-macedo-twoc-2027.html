Cole Macedo Returning to TwoC Racing for Third World of Outlaws Season in 2027

The Lemoore, Calif., driver will stay with Todd Carlile’s team for a third straight season on the World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series.

Published November 10, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Cole Macedo will return to TwoC Racing’s No. 2C for the 2027 World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series season, sprintcarunlimited.com reported on Nov. 7 during the World Finals at The Dirt Track at Charlotte. The 2027 season will be his third with the team owned by Todd Carlile.

World of Outlaws statistics credit Macedo with 74 feature starts in 2026, with eight top-five finishes, 29 top-10s, four quick times and 73 laps led. He is listed eighth on the series’ 2026 driver page, with $172,975 in earnings. That compares with 59 starts, six top fives and 21 top 10s in his 2025 rookie season. Macedo has not yet won a World of Outlaws feature; his career totals with the series stand at 180 starts, 19 top fives and 63 top 10s.

Macedo joined TwoC Racing for 2025, with 2020 Kevin Gobrecht Rookie of the Year Wayne Johnson moving from driver to crew chief on the No. 2C, according to the World of Outlaws. In April 2026, the series reported the team had recovered from multiple crashes at Kennedale Speedway Park to post a run of strong finishes, including a front-row start alongside his older brother, Carson Macedo, at US 36 Raceway. Before joining the tour, Cole Macedo won with the All Star Circuit of Champions while driving for Ray Brooks Racing and drove the Tarlton Motorsports No. 21 on the West Coast.

The 2027 World of Outlaws season begins Feb. 10-13 at Volusia Speedway Park’s Federated Auto Parts DIRTcar Nationals.
