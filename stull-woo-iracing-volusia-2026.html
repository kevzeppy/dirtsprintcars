Aiden Stull Wins Opening 2026 World of Outlaws iRacing Pro Series Sprint Car Race at Volusia

Stull led the 35-driver field to victory in the first of five races for the 2026 Sprint Car Championship. He holds a one-point lead over Alex Bergeron heading to Huset's Speedway on Nov. 23.

Published November 17, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Aiden Stull scored the opening victory of the 2026 World of Outlaws Pro Series Sprint Car Championship on iRacing Monday night at Volusia Speedway Park, according to World of Outlaws and iRacing reports.

The series returned for its first of five races, with 35 drivers who qualified through an earlier Qualifying Series. A $25,000 purse is on the line for the Sprint Car Championship.

Stull worked his way forward and held off challengers to take his first career win in the series. Alex Bergeron finished second, and Tanner Pettit was third after a late incident. Stull leads the championship standings by a single point over Bergeron, with Pettit third.

The second round heads to Huset's Speedway on Nov. 23. Landon Cardoza scored his first career win there last season, while Ryan Avila was the first winner at the virtual track two seasons ago, iRacing noted.

Sources: Aiden Stull Kicks Off 2026 World of Outlaws iRacing Pro Series Sprint Car Championship With Volusia Win, World of Outlaws (Nov. 17, 2026); Aiden Stull kicks off 2026 World of Outlaws Pro Series Sprint Car Championship with maiden win at Volusia, iRacing.com (Nov. 17, 2026).
