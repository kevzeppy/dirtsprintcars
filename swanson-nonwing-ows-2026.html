Kody Swanson Wins Inaugural Non-Wing Feature at Open Wheel Showdown

Kody Swanson added another Open Wheel Showdown trophy, taking the $10,000 inaugural non-winged sprint car feature at the Bullring in Las Vegas. No wing, no problem.

Published November 7, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Who needs a wing when you've got Kody Swanson? The multi-time USAC Silver Crown champ and Open Wheel Showdown regular won Friday night's inaugural Elliott's Custom Trailers & Carts Non-Winged sprint car feature at the OnlyBulls Open Wheel Showdown at the Bullring at Las Vegas Motor Speedway, banking the $10,000 prize.

Swanson started second, took the lead on lap two, traded the top spot with Jackson Macenko in traffic, then reclaimed it for good on lap 42 and drove away to a 6.5-second win over Evan Margeson. Chris Muhler, Michael Fanelli and Austyn Gossel rounded out the top five in the 75-lapper.

The win adds to Swanson's 2023 midget and 2024-2025 winged sprint car triumphs at the event. He called it a special challenge on new pavement in a new division, and a nice one for car owner Nick Bohanan. Saturday's program continues the pavement party.

Sources: SPEED SPORT (Nov. 7, 2026).
