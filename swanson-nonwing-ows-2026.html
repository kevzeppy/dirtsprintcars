Kody Swanson Wins Non-Winged Feature at Open Wheel Showdown

Kody Swanson added another Open Wheel Showdown victory, taking the $10,000 inaugural non-winged sprint car feature at the Bullring at Las Vegas Motor Speedway on Friday night.

Published November 8, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Kody Swanson continues to dominate the OnlyBulls Open Wheel Showdown. On Friday night at the Bullring at Las Vegas Motor Speedway, Swanson won the inaugural Elliott’s Custom Trailers & Carts Non-Winged sprint car feature, leading the 75-lap race to the $10,000 prize.

Swanson, who previously won the midget feature in 2023 and the winged sprint car features in 2024 and 2025, started near the front and took the lead early. He and Jackson Macenko traded the top spot in traffic before Swanson reclaimed control on the high side on Lap 42 and drove away to a 6.5-second victory. Evan Margeson finished second, with Chris Muhler third.

The non-winged class drew a strong field of 59 cars for its debut at the event, highlighting the growing interest in pavement non-winged sprint car racing. Swanson called it a special event and a lot of work over the three days.

Sources: SPEED SPORT (Nov. 7, 2026).
