Nov 20: James McFadden Wins Australian Sprintcar Championship Night 1 at Toowoomba

McFadden led the feature on the opening night of the 64th Nutrend Homes Australian Sprintcar Championship at Hi-Tec Oils Toowoomba Speedway. Ryan Newton finished second and Brock Hallett third, with Lachlan McHugh fourth.

Published November 20, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

James McFadden won Night 1 of the Australian Sprintcar Championship at Hi-Tec Oils Toowoomba Speedway on Friday, November 20, 2026. The result was reported by observers on social media following the race.

The finishing order saw McFadden first, Ryan Newton second, Brock Hallett third, Lachlan McHugh fourth, and Liam Williams fifth. This is the first night of the two-night championship event, the first time the title has been contested at Toowoomba.

The series continues Saturday night with the championship feature. Source attribution is provided below pending full official results.

Source: Social media reports and race observers (e.g., Rick Hunt / @rickgoalie52, Nov. 20, 2026). Official results pending from series organizers. Previous night (Prelude): Lachlan McHugh win via Speedcafe.
