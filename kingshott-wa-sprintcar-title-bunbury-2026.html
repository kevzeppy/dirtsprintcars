Dayne Kingshott Wins WA Sprintcar Title at Bunbury

Kingshott led all 35 laps from second at Bunbury Speedway on Nov. 7, delivering Krikke Motorsport its ninth Western Australian Sprintcar Championship and his second title.

Published November 10, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Dayne Kingshott won the Western Australian Sprintcar Championship on Saturday, Nov. 7, at Bunbury Speedway, leading every lap of the 35-lap feature after starting second. The result was Krikke Motorsport’s ninth WA title and the team’s first win of the 2026-27 season.

It was Kingshott’s second WA title. He previously won the championship at Perth Motorplex in the 2022-23 season. Auto Action and the team said it was also his first victory with Krikke Motorsport at the team’s home track. He drove the Mobil 1 and Bunbury Toyota-backed Maxim, the WA No. 2.

Callum Williamson finished second, followed by Kaiden Manders, Kris Coyle and Daniel Harding. Kingshott said, “It was awesome to win another Western Australian Title for the Krikke Motorsport team and what made it even more special for all of us was that it was our first win together at Bunbury.” He added that winning at the home track “hits differently” because of family, friends and local sponsors.

Team manager Ryan Krikke said he was pleased to get the team’s first win together at Bunbury and another Western Australian title. He said the team has had a good start to the season “but just haven’t got a lot to show for it so far,” and hoped the title win would build momentum toward a fourth Maddington Toyota Sprintcar Series championship. Kingshott sits seventh in the MTSS standings heading into Saturday’s King of Wings at Perth Motorplex, an event he has won the past three years.

Sources: Krikke Motorsport (Nov. 9, 2026); Auto Action (Andrew Clarke, Nov. 10, 2026).
