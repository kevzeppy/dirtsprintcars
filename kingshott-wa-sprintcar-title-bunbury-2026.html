Nov 9: Dayne Kingshott Wins WA Sprintcar Title at Bunbury

Dayne Kingshott led every lap of the 35-lap championship feature at Bunbury Speedway on Nov. 7 to deliver Krikke Motorsport its ninth Western Australian Sprintcar title. He started second and finished ahead of Callum Williamson and Kaiden Manders. Sources: Auto Action; The West Australian / South Western Times.
