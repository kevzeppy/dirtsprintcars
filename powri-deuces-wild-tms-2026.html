Nov 3: POWRi Deuces Wild Championship Weekend Hits TMS Dirt Track

The POWRi Honest Abe Roofing 410 Outlaw Sprint League closes its 2026 season at the Texas Motor Speedway Dirt Track, November 5-7, joined by Heartland Late Models and USRA Modifieds.

Published November 3, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Qualifying Thursday, full shows Friday and Saturday. Stacked field of regulars plus outsiders like Jack Dover, Tanner Thorson and more. Texas, are you ready? The dirt track under the lights at TMS has a way of turning notes from last year into last-lap heroics (or heartbreaks). Dover's aiming for a couple top-fives in his POWRi debut weekend — the Lone Star State doesn't hand those out with the barbecue.
