Daison Pursley Won the Last Hangtown 100 and Is Already a USAC Midget Champion at 22

The Locust Grove, Okla., driver won the most recent Hangtown 100, the opener of USAC’s California midget swing, five years after a crash that left him paralyzed from the neck down.

Published November 10, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com
Update, Nov. 12: The Hangtown 100 was canceled because of forecast storms. Read the story. The article below was published before the cancellation.

The USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midgets finish the 2026 season with seven races in California, the Golden State Invasion, starting with the Hangtown 100 at Placerville Speedway on Nov. 13-14. The swing continues at Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia on Nov. 18, the track’s first date on the series schedule, then the Chase Johnson Classic at Merced Speedway on Nov. 20-21, the Jason Leffler Memorial at Bakersfield Speedway at Kevin Harvick’s Kern Raceway on Nov. 24 and the 85th Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura Raceway on Nov. 28, according to USAC’s schedule. The most recent Hangtown 100, in 2025, was won by Daison Pursley of Locust Grove, Okla., who turned 22 on Oct. 20. The 2024 edition was canceled for rain and cold.

Pursley started fourth in a 25-car field on Nov. 15, 2025, and stayed in the top four for all 100 laps. On lap 96 he was second and tried to pass leader Kyle Larson in turn one; Pursley’s car appeared to clip the infield berm and slid into Larson’s, which flipped. Pursley finished 0.257 seconds ahead of Steven Snyder Jr. for $20,000, USAC reported. He apologized afterward and said he had not wanted to win that way.

The win was one entry on a long USAC list. Pursley won the National Midget championship in 2024 with CB Industries and the Indiana Midget Week title the same year, and he was the 2023 National Sprint Car Rookie of the Year, the 2023 and 2024 National Passing Master champion and the 2017 National Micro Sprint champion, according to USAC’s driver page. That page, last updated on Jan. 1, 2026, credits him with 11 National Midget wins in 136 starts through 2025, seven of them in 2024. At the 2025 4-Crown Nationals at Eldora Speedway he won the National Sprint Car, National Midget and Silver Crown features in one night, joining Jack Hewitt, Larson and Logan Seavey as the fourth driver to do so.

His career nearly ended at 17. A crash at Arizona Speedway in November 2021 broke his neck, and he was diagnosed as an incomplete quadriplegic. After surgery and rehabilitation at the Shepherd Center in Atlanta, he attended the 2022 Chili Bowl Nationals in Tulsa that January, returned to racing in August 2022 and was back in victory lane that September, according to KJRH and FloRacing. He finished second to Larson in the 2025 Chili Bowl Nationals and sixth in the 2026 A-feature.

Pursley has also moved into winged sprint cars. He won at Lernerville Speedway on Sept. 13, 2025, and was named the High Limit Racing Rookie of the Year driving for Buch Motorsports. In October 2025 Kasey Kahne Racing hired him as its full-time driver of the No. 9 for 2026, after Brad Sweet stepped away from full-time touring, FloRacing reported, and Placerville Speedway’s preview for this week’s race calls him a High Limit regular. On Sept. 26, 2026, he led all 50 laps in the USAC Silver Crown feature at the 4-Crown Nationals, the debut of the Spire Motorsports/CB Industries Silver Crown team, for his fifth career 4-Crown win as a driver, Speed Sport reported.
