Oct 27: Allard Lays Down Early Title Marker at Baypark

Two-time New Zealand Sprint Car champion Jonathan Allard sent an early warning with a commanding victory at Baypark Speedway’s season-opening meeting. The California native held off Dean Brindle by 0.893 seconds. Because nothing says "I'm back" like winning the first race of the season and making the other guys sweat already.
