Kale Drake Joins RMS Racing for 2026 USAC National Midget Season

The Collinsville, Oklahoma driver will run the full 29-race USAC NOS Energy Drink Midget National Championship tour in the RMS No. 4 after two-plus seasons with Keith Kunz/Curb-Agajanian.

Published November 11, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Kale Drake has a new home for the 2026 USAC NOS Energy Drink Midget National Championship. The 20-year-old will pilot the RMS Racing No. 4 on the full national tour after competing the past two-plus seasons with Keith Kunz/Curb-Agajanian Motorsports.

Drake is the 2025 USAC Indiana Midget Week champion. He has made 46 career series starts and won three features over the past two seasons, including two in 2025 at Sweet Springs and Circle City. He finished fifth in series points last year.

RMS has won 22 career USAC National Midget features, including the Turkey Night Grand Prix, BC39 and Hangtown 100. Drake is eager to start the season with a team that has a proven record at the sport’s biggest events.

He will open his 2026 campaign with back-to-back nights at Kokomo Speedway for the Kokomo Grand Prix on April 24-25.

Source: USAC Racing (Nov. 10, 2026).
