Keith Kunz Motorsports to close after 2026 season

Keith Kunz has announced that his team will close at the end of the season, and says he plans to join 4 Kings Racing.

Published October 20, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Keith Kunz has announced that Keith Kunz Motorsports will close at the end of the 2026 season.

Kunz made the announcement in an interview with Conner Wade, published October 20 by DIRTVision.

In the interview, Kunz explains the decision, reflects on what led his team to success, and discusses his future plans, which include joining 4 Kings Racing.

Source: DIRTVision interview with Keith Kunz. DirtSprintCars.com has not independently confirmed the details beyond the interview.
