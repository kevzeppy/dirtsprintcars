Tanner Thorson Wins Trophy Cup Finale; Landon Brooks Takes Trophy Cup Title

Thorson won the 50-lap finale from 15th. Brooks finished 4th from 22nd to win the Trophy Cup 32 points title.

Published November 1, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Tanner Thorson won the 50-lap 410 winged sprint car A-main in the Trophy Cup 32 finale on Saturday, Oct. 31, at Thunderbowl Raceway in Tulare, Calif. Thorson started 15th and earned $10,000. He also won Night 2 on Oct. 30.

Landon Brooks started 22nd and finished 4th to win the overall Trophy Cup 32 points championship, worth $30,000.

Tanner Holmes, who finished 3rd, won the Three Cup Challenge championship.

Top 5: 1. Tanner Thorson, 2. Jett Barnes, 3. Tanner Holmes, 4. Landon Brooks, 5. Justin Peck.

Sources: SPEED SPORT and TJ Slideways for the finale and championship results. The Three Cup Challenge result is from the event results.
