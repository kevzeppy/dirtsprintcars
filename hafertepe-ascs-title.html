Oct 18: Hafertepe Jr. locks record seventh ASCS national title

Source: American Sprint Car Series

Sam Hafertepe Jr. captured a record seventh ASCS National Tour championship in 2026, wrapping the season at the awards banquet in Tulsa. The Sunnyvale, Texas veteran added another title to a career that already had him in the conversation with the series greats.

Seven national titles. At some point the trophy case needs its own ZIP code. Hafertepe just keeps showing up and making the rest of the field figure out how to finish second.
