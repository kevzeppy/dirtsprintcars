Update: USAC Reschedules Plaza Park National Midget Debut for Nov. 23

The Good Earth Farms Special, postponed from Wednesday, Nov. 18, because of rain, will be run Monday night at Visalia, Calif. It is round three of the Golden State Invasion and USAC’s national debut at the 1/5-mile oval.

Published November 18, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com
Update, Nov. 19: USAC canceled Friday’s Merced program because of wet grounds and consolidated the Chase Johnson Classic into Saturday, Nov. 21. Read the story.

USAC has rescheduled the first USAC NOS Energy Drink Midget National Championship event at Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia, California, for Monday night, Nov. 23, the series announced Nov. 17. The original Wednesday, Nov. 18, date for the Good Earth Farms Special Presented by Peelz was postponed because of rain, according to USAC.

The Plaza Park event will be round three of USAC’s Golden State Invasion and marks the series’ national debut at the 1/5-mile dirt oval, USAC said. A-Class Micros are also on the card. The feature is 40 laps and pays $5,000 to win.

On race day, the pits open at 3:30 p.m. Pacific, the front gate opens at 5 p.m., the drivers meeting is at 5:15 p.m. and hot laps begin at 6 p.m., followed by qualifying and racing, according to USAC. Advance tickets are on sale at plazaparkraceway.com or on the Ticket Hoss app, and tickets will also be sold at the gate. Adult general admission (ages 13 and up) is $30, senior and military general admission are $20, children ages 4 and under are free, and pit passes are $45, USAC said.

The Golden State Invasion consists of five USAC NOS Energy Drink Midget National Championship events in California, starting Friday and Saturday, Nov. 20-21, with the Chase Johnson Classic at Merced Speedway, then continuing at Plaza Park on Monday, Nov. 23, according to USAC. The series then goes to Bakersfield Speedway at Kevin Harvick’s Kern Raceway on Tuesday, Nov. 24, for the Jason Leffler Memorial and finishes Saturday, Nov. 28, at Ventura Raceway with the 85th running of the ARP Turkey Night Grand Prix Presented by the West Coast Stock Car Hall of Fame.
