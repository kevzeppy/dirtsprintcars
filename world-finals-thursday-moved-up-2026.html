Nov 4: World Finals Thursday Program Moved Up One Hour

Rain trying to crash the party at Charlotte, so World of Outlaws bumps the Thursday schedule an hour earlier to get the dirt flying before the sky opens.

Published November 4, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

To help ensure fans and race teams get a full race program for the Thursday portion of the Dave Smith Motors World of Outlaws World Finals, event times have been moved up an hour to avoid a potential threat of rain later in the day.

Opening ceremonies are now scheduled to start at 2:50 p.m. (ET), with racing to follow at 3 p.m. The midway Fan Zone will still open at 12 p.m. The World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series will be the first division on track, followed by the World of Outlaws Late Models presented by DIRTVision, and then the Super DIRTcar Series. They will stay in that order throughout the entire program on Thursday.

Friday and Saturday’s programs remain unchanged. For further updates, follow the World Finals, World of Outlaws, and Super DIRTcar Series social media channels.

The four-night event at The Dirt Track at Charlotte features the sprint cars, late models and big-block modifieds all on the same track. Gravel is chasing a historic three-peat while the rest of the field looks to spoil the party.
