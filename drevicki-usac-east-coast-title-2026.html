Steven Drevicki Wins Sixth USAC East Coast Sprint Car Title

The Reading, Pennsylvania driver claimed his record-extending sixth USAC East Coast Sprint Cars Presented by Baer Den Farms championship, 28 points ahead of Christian Bruno.

Published November 10, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Steven Drevicki of Reading, Pennsylvania won his record-extending sixth career USAC East Coast Sprint Cars Presented by Baer Den Farms championship in 2026. The 35-year-old closed out the closest points race in series history, finishing 28 points ahead of Christian Bruno of Deptford, New Jersey, 847-819. The previous closest title fight was 44 points in the series’ inaugural 2018 season, also won by Drevicki.

All six of Drevicki’s titles have come in his own No. 19. He won the first three series championships in 2018, 2019 and 2020, then completed a second three-peat in 2024, 2025 and 2026. He recorded two feature victories, both at Bridgeport Motorsports Park in New Jersey, a series-high 11 top-five finishes, and top-10 results in all 12 of his feature starts. After chasing the point lead for much of the season, he took over the top spot from Bruno with three races remaining and closed with a fourth, a win and a sixth in his final three starts.

Drevicki credited a midseason stretch of top-five finishes in Pennsylvania and New Jersey, including a wire-to-wire win at Bridgeport in August in which he led all 25 laps. In a September return to Bridgeport, he slid J.R. Fulper for the lead in a three-car battle with two laps remaining; Fulper’s car then began to stumble without fuel. “It’s wild to think about six,” Drevicki said. “I don’t know that it’s even sunk in.”

Five drivers won USAC East Coast features in 2026: Bruno, Briggs Danner, Drevicki, Kenny Miller III and Chelby Hinton. Bruno led the series with five victories — Big Diamond Speedway in May, a three-race sweep at Selinsgrove Speedway, and Port Royal Speedway in September — and also led the tour in laps led (69) and heat-race wins (7). He finished second in the standings for the second time in the last three years. Miller was third, Kyle Spence fourth and Joe Kata fifth, both Spence and Kata posting their best career series points finishes. Olivia Thayer was named Rookie of the Year. Four drivers started all 12 features: Bruno, Drevicki, Kata and Miller.
