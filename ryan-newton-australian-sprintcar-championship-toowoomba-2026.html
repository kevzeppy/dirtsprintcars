Ryan Newton Wins Australian Sprintcar Championship at Toowoomba

Newton passed Lachlan McHugh in the closing laps of the 40-lap A Main at Hi-Tec Oils Toowoomba Speedway on Saturday to win his first Australian 410 title. Brock Hallett finished second and McHugh third.

Published November 21, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Ryan Newton won the 64th Nutrend Homes Australian Sprintcar Championship on Saturday, Nov. 21, at Hi-Tec Oils Toowoomba Speedway in Toowoomba, Queensland, according to Speedcafe. Newton, driving the Q66, beat Brock Hallett (Q5) by 1.974 seconds in the 40-lap A Main, according to the event’s Race Monitor timing and scoring. Lachlan McHugh (NQ7) finished third, Brendan Quinn (S37) fourth and reigning champion Jock Goodyer (A1) fifth. Speedcafe reported it was Newton’s first Australian 410 Sprintcar title and that he became the first driver to hold the 410 and LS/360 Australian titles at the same time.

Newton had a difficult qualifying session on Night 1 and had to race through a heat on Night 2, where he finished second to Goodyer, Speedcafe said. That put him into the Mid Pack Dash on points. He finished second there to transfer to the Stars Dash, which set the first four rows of the A Main, and finished third in the Stars Dash behind McHugh and Night 1 winner James McFadden.

McHugh and McFadden swapped the lead on the opening lap, according to Speedcafe. McFadden’s title bid ended with 27 laps remaining when contact with a lapped car deflated his right-rear tire. With 15 laps to go, McHugh made contact with the lapped car of Randy Morgan on the back straight, but both continued. One lap later the race was stopped when Matthew Dumesny hit the wall and went onto his side after Goodyer moved across his nose entering turn 3. Luke Oldfield, who had been racing in the top five, pulled into the infield with a flat right-rear tire during the caution.

McHugh pulled away again on the restart, but Newton caught him inside the final 10 laps, and the two swapped sides of the track for several laps, Speedcafe reported. McHugh drifted into the cushion in two straight corners inside the final five laps, letting Newton clear him. Hallett took second from McHugh with two laps to go. McHugh had led more than three quarters of the race. Goodyer won the second B Main and started 16th in the A Main before finishing fifth. Aaron Kelly (Q7) was sixth, Daniel Sayre (N16) seventh, Max Guilford (N79) eighth, Marcus Dumesny (N47) ninth and Morgan (Q54) 10th.

“It’s unbelievable, I’ve got so many people to thank,” Newton told Speedcafe. “We’ve been working super hard, me and Damon, it’s finally paid off.” Speedcafe reported that it was the first time Toowoomba had hosted the championship, in the speedway’s 50th year of operation.

Sources: Ryan Newton makes history with dual Sprintcar titles, Jack Fletcher, Speedcafe (Nov. 21, 2026); Australian Championship A Main timing and scoring, Race Monitor (Nov. 21, 2026); event guide, Speedcafe (Nov. 18, 2026). No permitted photo was available from this race.
