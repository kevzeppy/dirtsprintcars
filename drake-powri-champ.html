Oct 25: Kale Drake clinches POWRi National Midget League championship

Source: Speedway Digest / Toyota Racing

Kale Drake and the RMS Racing team locked up the 2026 POWRi National Midget League title at I-44 Riverside Speedway. Four wins and 16 top-fives in his first full season with the team—first national midget crown for both driver and car owner. Toyota kept the streak alive too: third straight POWRi title for a Toyota-powered driver.

Drake is now the first driver in POWRi history to win both the National Midget and Micro League championships. Not a bad resume for a 21-year-old from Collinsville, Oklahoma. The Chili Bowl is next on the calendar, because why rest when there's another big race?
