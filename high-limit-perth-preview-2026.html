High Limit International Returns to Perth Motorplex Dec. 28-30

The third annual High Limit International is set for December 28-30, 2026, at Western Australia’s Perth Motorplex.

The event features two prelims paying $20,000 to win and a record-setting $120,000-to-win AUD finale, the highest-paying event in Australian sprint car racing history. Tickets are on sale at motorplex.com.au.

Previous editions drew sellout crowds, with Kyle Larson winning both. The series returns after its 2026 U.S. campaign concluded with Rico Abreu’s back-to-back championship.

Sources: High Limit Racing; Perth Motorplex; MyRacePass.
