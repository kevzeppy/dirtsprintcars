Blake Hahn Wins POWRi 410 Season Finale at Texas Motor Speedway

Hahn led all 25 laps from the pole on Saturday, Nov. 7, for his first career POWRi Honest Abe Roofing 410 Outlaw Sprint League feature win on Championship Night of Deuces Wild.

Published November 8, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Blake Hahn closed the 2026 POWRi Honest Abe Roofing 410 Outlaw Sprint League season with a wire-to-wire win Saturday, Nov. 7, at Texas Motor Speedway Dirt Track. Starting from the pole as the high-point qualifier, Hahn led every lap of the 25-lap Championship Night feature in Deuces Wild for his first career victory in the series.

Austin McCarl, who started fifth, finished second after a late charge. Friday night’s winner Rees Moran was third, with Brenham Crouch fourth and Xavier Doney fifth. SPEED SPORT and STLRacing reported 25 entries. Ryder McCutcheon set quick time in Thursday qualifying at 13.516 seconds and started second; he finished eighth. Heat winners were Moran, Hahn and Justin Henderson. Joe B. Miller was the hard charger, advancing seven positions.

Hahn said he tagged the wall early but the car stayed together, then held off McCarl on a late restart by running the high side. “This car was really good tonight. I tagged the wall early and thought I gave it away, but it stayed together,” Hahn said. “I saw Austin coming late, and on that last restart I knew I had to hit my marks on top and just hammer down. A great way to cap off the year and to be able to get a win here at TMS in a 410 is special.”

Sources: SPEED SPORT (Nov. 8, 2026); STLRacing.com (Nov. 8, 2026, datelined Nov. 7).
