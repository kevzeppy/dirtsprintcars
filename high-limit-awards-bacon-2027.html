Nov 14: High Limit Championship Awards Conclude 2026; Bacon Set for Full-Time 2027 with Vermeer

Interstate Batteries High Limit Racing wrapped its 2026 season with a Championship Awards Celebration. Rico Abreu claimed his second straight title.
