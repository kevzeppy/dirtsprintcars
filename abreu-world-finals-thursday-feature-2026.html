Rico Abreu Wins Thursday World Finals Feature at Charlotte

Rico Abreu started third and won Thursday’s 25-lap NOS Energy Drink feature at the Dave Smith Motors World of Outlaws World Finals at The Dirt Track at Charlotte. Tyler Courtney finished second and Bill Balog third.

Published November 5, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Rico Abreu followed his qualifying sweep with a feature win Thursday at Charlotte. Abreu, in the Tony Stewart/Curb-Agajanian Racing No. 14, started third in the 25-lap World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series main and finished ahead of Tyler Courtney and Bill Balog. Michael “Buddy” Kofoid ran fourth from sixth and Garet Williamson finished fifth after starting on the pole. David Gravel was sixth from fifth.

Abreu set the night up earlier in the program. He won the WIX Filters heat from the front row and finished third in the six-lap Toyota Dash, which Williamson won over Balog. Courtney started fourth in the feature after finishing fourth in the dash. The other heat winners were Gravel, Balog and Kofoid. Logan Schuchart, Giovanni Scelzi, Logan Rumsey (from 16th) and Spencer Bayston (from 15th) completed the top 10. Parker Price-Miller won the Last Chance Showdown and Emerson Axsom won the C-main. Conner Morrell, Buddy Schweibinz, Sheldon Haudenschild and Skylar Gee were listed as DNFs.

Abreu came to Charlotte off the 2026 High Limit Racing championship. On Wednesday’s qualifying night he set overall Simpson Quick Time for both Thursday (12.584) and Friday (12.542) heat lineups. Gravel came into the week going for a third straight World of Outlaws title with Big Game Motorsports. Feature points from Thursday and Friday will set heat races for Saturday’s $25,000-to-win season finale.

Balog led 21 of the 25 laps on the cushion before Abreu got a run off the bottom of Turn 2 with fewer than five laps left and drove by for the lead, according to World of Outlaws. Abreu led the final four laps and won by 1.136 seconds over Courtney, earning $15,000. World of Outlaws said the win was Abreu’s 11th sprint car victory of 2026 and his second at Charlotte, and the 332nd World of Outlaws win for Tony Stewart’s operation, 14 of them at the Concord track. The World Finals continue Friday with another $15,000-to-win sprint car program.

Sources: World of Outlaws (official Thursday recap and feature results, Nov. 5, 2026; qualifying, Nov. 4, 2026); Racing News (Thursday heat, dash, C-main and LCS results, Nov. 5, 2026).
