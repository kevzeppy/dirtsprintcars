Jason Martin Healthy and Open to Driving or Crew Roles After DJ Bybee Steps Back

Martin says he is fully recovered after sitting out 2026 and open to driving, crew, or team opportunities. BM Motorsports is not closing.

Published November 9, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Jason Martin says he is fully recovered and open to driving, crew, or team opportunities after DJ Bybee stepped back from BM Motorsports.

Martin announced the change Monday. Bybee, a longtime partner and primary sponsor, is shifting his focus to growing Bybee Electric. Martin thanked Bybee and said the partnership was built on friendship as well as racing.

Martin, a longtime 360 sprint car driver based in the Lincoln, Nebraska area, is the 2023 American Sprint Car Series National Tour champion. He has more than 100 career feature wins and has raced in 34 states and Canada. He and Bybee formed BM Motorsports in 2017.

Martin sat out the 2026 season after injuries sustained in April. He wrote that he is “100% healed” and “ready to get back behind the wheel.” During the year off he worked as crew chief while Jake Bubak drove the car.

He is open to:

  • Driving for an established team
  • A hired mechanic, crew chief, or team management role
  • A funded driver joining the existing BM Motorsports program

Martin said the team is not closing. “This isn’t about closing a chapter on racing. It’s about being open-minded, finding the right opportunities, and figuring out the best way to keep doing what I love.”

Sources: BM Motorsports / Jason Martin announcement (Nov. 9, 2026); Jason Martin Motorsports bio; ASCS Racing.
