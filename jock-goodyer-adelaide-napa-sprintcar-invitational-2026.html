Jock Goodyer Leads Every Lap to Win Adelaide NAPA Sprintcar Invitational Feature

Two-time Australian champion Jock Goodyer led from start to finish in the 35-lap A Main on Friday, Nov. 27, the final Sprintcar night of the NAPA Sprintcar Invitational at the temporary NAPA Speedway in Adelaide. Marcus Dumesny was second and James McFadden third.

Published November 27, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Jock Goodyer led every lap to win the 35-lap A Main on Friday night, Nov. 27, the final night of Sprintcar racing at the NAPA Sprintcar Invitational, billed as Sprintcars in the City, at the temporary NAPA Speedway in Adelaide, South Australia, according to Speedcafe. Goodyer (T22) finished ahead of Marcus Dumesny (N47), with James McFadden (WX23) third, Lachlan McHugh (NQ7) fourth and Brock Hallett (Q5) fifth. Speedcafe reported it was the two-time Australian champion’s second win on the temporary Adelaide speedway and came one week after he relinquished the Australian title.

The Dumesny brothers had looked like the pair to beat across both nights, with Matt Dumesny winning two heat races and a Preliminary A Main, but Goodyer dominated Friday afternoon’s qualifying shootout ahead of the feature, Speedcafe reported. Thirty-one laps were run under green, and the leaders were in lapped traffic from very early in the race. Brendan Quinn had moved into the top five before he caught the inside kerb, bounced into the wall and pulled the left rear tire off the wheel with 12 laps to go. With 10 laps left, McFadden and McHugh made contact in Turn 4 while racing for third, which let Marcus Dumesny pull away for a short time. McHugh had caught Dumesny again with five laps to go when a yellow flag flew on the next lap for Matt Egel, who was sidelined with a flat right rear tire. Goodyer pulled away on the restart, and McFadden passed McHugh around the outside for third and nearly caught Dumesny for second in the closing laps.

“We didn’t qualify very well and we haven’t been doing that, I just keep digging and digging,” Goodyer said, according to Speedcafe. “I just knew in that heat I needed to get to second so I had a shot to get in that Shootout. I love those things, so I knew I could get it to the front.” Matt Dumesny (N57) dropped to sixth, ahead of Californian Joel Myers Jr. (USA46) in seventh and American Hunter Schuerenberg (V88) in eighth. Dylan Menz (Q46) was ninth and American Garet Williamson (V39) 10th. Speedcafe listed Ayrton Hodson (NQ28) as the leading of the two Kiwi guests in 13th, with New Zealander Max Guilford (NZ79) 14th.

The feature followed Thursday’s opening night, when Matthew Dumesny and Lachlan McHugh won the two Preliminary A Mains. Sprintcar racing at the event, part of the Supercars bp Adelaide Grand Final weekend, has concluded. According to Speedcafe, NAPA Speedway in the City continues Saturday and Sunday with daytime racing for Junior Formula 500s and Junior Sedans, along with Sprintcar demonstrations and two-seater rides.
