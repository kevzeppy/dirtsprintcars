High Limit Sets Freedom 251 Weekend at Mansfield Speedway for July 2-3, 2027

High Limit Racing will run a Lucas Oil Late Model Dirt Series doubleheader Friday, July 2, and a $51,000-to-win program with fireworks Saturday, July 3, at Mansfield Speedway in Mansfield, Ohio.

Published November 17, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

High Limit Racing will visit Mansfield Speedway in Mansfield, Ohio, for the track’s Freedom 251 weekend on Friday, July 2, and Saturday, July 3, 2027, the series announced Tuesday. According to High Limit, Friday’s program is a Lucas Oil Late Model Dirt Series and High Limit doubleheader. Saturday’s High Limit race pays $51,000 to win and includes fireworks.

A Mansfield Speedway graphic posted with the announcement lists Friday, July 2, 2027, with Interstate Batteries High Limit Racing and the Lucas Oil Late Model Dirt Series, and highlights Saturday, July 3, 2027, as the Freedom 251 date. July 2, 2027, is a Friday, and July 3 is a Saturday. The Lucas Oil Late Model Dirt Series, which unveiled its 2027 schedule Tuesday, also listed Mansfield Speedway on July 2 and said High Limit Racing will race alongside it.

High Limit’s announcement also noted that IndyCar races at Mid-Ohio on Sunday, July 4. That race is not part of the High Limit weekend at Mansfield.

Sources: High Limit Racing post and Mansfield Speedway graphic (Nov. 17, 2026); Lucas Oil Late Model Dirt Series Unveils Expanded 2027 Schedule, Lucas Oil Late Model Dirt Series (Nov. 17, 2026).
