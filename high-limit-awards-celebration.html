Oct 28: High Limit Championship Awards wrap 2026 season as Abreu celebrates back-to-back titles

Source: High Limit Racing

After 54 events across 19 states, Interstate Batteries High Limit Racing threw a proper Championship Awards Celebration in Fort Worth. Rico Abreu walked away with his second straight title (and a couple of speeches that had the room laughing), while the High Roller Club teams pocketed north of $2.6 million on top of their weekly purses. That's a lot of wing nuts and hotel mini-bars.

Next up: High Limit International returns to Perth Motorplex in December with a record $120k-to-win AUD finale. Because apparently the season never really ends when the money's this good.
