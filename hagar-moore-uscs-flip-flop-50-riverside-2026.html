Derek Hagar and Howard Moore Win USCS Flip Flop 50 Features at Riverside

Hagar won the Flip from fifth and finished third in the Flop after starting 23rd. Moore was second in the Flip and won the Flop from 10th at West Memphis, Ark., on Saturday, Nov. 21.

Published November 22, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Derek Hagar won the first 25-lap feature and Howard Moore won the second at the ACE DORAN 18th Annual Bobby Martin Memorial USCS Flip Flop 50 at Riverside International Speedway in West Memphis, Arkansas, on Saturday, Nov. 21, according to results posted by Hoseheads.com. SPEED SPORT published the same finishing orders on Sunday, Nov. 22. An official United Sprint Car Series race report was not found Sunday evening.

Hagar started fifth in the first feature, known as the Flip, and won ahead of Moore, Riley Goodno, Brad Bowden and Hayden Martin, according to the posted results. Polesitter Landon Crawley finished 21st in the 23-car field. Under the format announced by Riverside International Speedway, the lineup for the second feature, the Flop, was inverted. Each feature paid $2,500 to win, with a $2,500 bonus for winning both. Riverside said Hagar is the only driver to have swept both features, which he did in 2024.

Moore started 10th and won the Flop, according to the posted results. Conner Wray started on the pole and finished second. Hagar started 23rd and finished third, Bowden was fourth for the second time of the night, and Keith Ainsworth was fifth.

Terry Gray started 10th and finished 18th in the Flip, then started 17th and finished 23rd in the Flop, according to the posted results. Riverside International Speedway said in a Facebook post before the event that the Flip Flop 50 would be the final competition of Gray’s career. USCS said Gray’s most recent series win, the 99th of his career, came Sept. 5 in the Randy Helton Memorial at Dixie Speedway in Woodstock, Georgia.

In Friday’s 15-lap pole scramble, Crawley won ahead of Gunner Swindell Herrington and Martin, and Gray finished 10th, according to results posted by Hoseheads.com.

Sources: Saturday and Friday results posted by Hoseheads.com (Nov. 20 and 21, 2026); SPEED SPORT (Nov. 22, 2026, Saturday finishing orders); Riverside International Speedway event announcement (format and purse); USCS (Gray’s 99th win, Sept. 5, 2026). An official USCS race report was not found Sunday evening, Nov. 22.
