Derek Hagar and Howard Moore Win USCS Flip Flop 50 Features as Terry Gray Clinches 17th Title

Hagar won the Flip from fifth and Moore won the Flop from 10th at Riverside International Speedway, while Gray clinched his 17th USCS National Championship in the final race of his career.

Published November 22, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Derek Hagar of Marion, Arkansas, and Howard Moore of Memphis, Tennessee, won the two 25-lap features in the 18th annual Ace Doran Bobby Martin Memorial Flip Flop 50 for the United Sprint Car Series at Riverside International Speedway in West Memphis, Arkansas, on Saturday, Nov. 21, according to the official USCS race report and results released by Roby Helm of Helm Communications and posted by Riverside International Speedway.

National Sprint Car Hall of Fame inductee Terry Gray of Bartlett, Tennessee, clinched his 17th USCS National Championship in the final race of the 2026 season and retired as an active competitor, according to the report. Gray won the title by 18 points over Jeff Willingham of Ripley, Mississippi, after entering the event with a 22-point lead. Gray was involved in a first-lap multi-car tangle in the Flip and restarted at the tail with a badly bent top wing. He finished 18th in the Flip after starting 10th, and 23rd in the Flop after starting 17th. “It’s been a lot of fun to get here, and I did it the way I wanted to,” Gray said in the report. “I wish my Mom and Dad were here, but I know they’re watching down from above and I have family here tonight.”

Hagar started fifth in the Flip, took the lead after contact with leader Gunner Swindell-Herrington on lap 10, and won by 1.537 seconds over Moore, according to the report. Riley Goodno, Brad Bowden and Hayden Martin completed the top five. Polesitter Landon Crawley finished 21st. Morgan Turpen-Havener started 21st and finished ninth to earn the Wilwood Disc Brakes Hard Charger Award.

The finish of the Flip set an inverted lineup for the Flop. Conner Wray led from the pole until Moore passed him on lap 21, and Moore won by .787 of a second, according to the report. Wray finished second, Hagar started 23rd and finished third to earn the Hard Charger Award, Bowden was fourth and Keith Ainsworth fifth. The report said Hagar is the only driver to win both races in the event’s 18-year history.

In Friday’s preliminary action, Crawley won the 15-lap pole scramble ahead of Swindell-Herrington and Martin, with Gray 10th. Heat races were won by Martin, Hagar, Dusty Young and Moore, and Bowden won Saturday’s 15-lap B-main, according to the official results. The event concluded the 2026 USCS season, the series’ 30th Anniversary Outlaw Thunder Tour. Earlier coverage of Gray’s final start is available on DirtSprintCars.com.

Sources: Official USCS race report and results, “Hagar and Moore Take USCS Flip Flop Wins, Champ Gray Calls It a Career,” by Roby Helm, Helm Communications, posted by Riverside International Speedway on Facebook (Nov. 22, 2026); SPEED SPORT (Nov. 22, 2026).
