D.J. Netto Wins Tribute to Gary Patterson; Justyn Cox Claims Second SCCT Title

Netto started second and won the Nov. 7 finale at the Stockton Dirt Track. Cox finished third to wrap up his second tour championship.

Published November 13, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

D.J. Netto of Hanford, California, won the Elk Grove Ford Sprint Car Challenge Tour season finale, the Tribute to Gary Patterson, on Saturday, Nov. 7, at the Stockton Dirt Track. The series said Netto started second in the Netto AG No. 88n and scored his second career Tribute to Gary Patterson victory, worth $5,000 with additional money from Noceti Group Inc. It was the fifth SCCT win of his career, tying him with Colby Copeland for fifth on the tour’s all-time list.

Officials shortened the main event to 25 laps after multiple red flags and cautions, according to the series recap. Pole sitter Carson Hammes, who had won the High Sierra Industries Dash, flipped at the start of the feature and brought out a red flag. The series said Hammes was uninjured but finished for the night. After the race was shortened, Netto led the rest of the way and lapped up to sixth place.

Shane Golobic of Fremont, California, finished second. Justyn Cox finished third and clinched his second Elk Grove Ford Sprint Car Challenge Tour championship. The series said Cox had two wins, eight top-five finishes and 12 top-10s on the way to the title. Jake Morgan finished fourth and Dylan Bloomfield fifth. Completing the top 10 were Andy Forsberg, Cole Croft, Jake Haulot, Dawson Hammes and hard charger Kenny Wanderstadt, who started 24th. Jett Barnes of Visalia set a new 360 track record at Stockton with a lap of 11.230, the series said.

Sources: Sprint Car Challenge Tour; SPEED SPORT.
