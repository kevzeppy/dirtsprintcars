Nov 10: Emerson Axsom Continues Full-Time Campaign in Klaasmeyer/Petry No. 27

The Franklin, Indiana driver is running his first full World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series campaign in the Klaasmeyer/Petry Motorsports No. 27. He scored his first series win earlier in 2026 at Huset’s Speedway. Sources: World of Outlaws; SPEED SPORT.
