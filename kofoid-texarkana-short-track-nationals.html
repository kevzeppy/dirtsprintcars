Oct 24: Kofoid capitalizes on late restart for Texarkana Short Track Nationals win

Source: World of Outlaws

Buddy Kofoid (that's Michael to the IRS) turned a late restart into his 17th World of Outlaws win of 2026 at Texarkana 67 Speedway. The Roth Motorsports No. 83 made it look easy after the caution, proving once again that when the track gets slick and the money's on the line, Kofoid is the guy you don't want to see in your mirror.

Cole Macedo finished second in his best Outlaws result yet, while Garet Williamson rounded out the podium. The win also locked up Roth Motorsports as the winningest team of the season—first time for the organization. Not bad for a guy whose first name the internet still argues about.
