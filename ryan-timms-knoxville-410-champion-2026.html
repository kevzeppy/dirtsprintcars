Ryan Timms Honored as Knoxville 410 Champion as 2027 Title Prize Doubles to $40,000

Timms was honored as a first-time champion at the 71st Knoxville Raceway awards banquet on Saturday, Nov. 21. Knoxville Raceway announced that the 2027 410 champion will receive $40,000, up from $20,000.

Published November 23, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Ryan Timms was honored as the 2026 Knoxville Championship Series champion in the 410 sprint car class, his first title in the division, at the 71st Knoxville Raceway awards banquet on Saturday, Nov. 21, at Prairie Meadows Racetrack and Casino in Altoona, Iowa, according to a banquet report by Bill Wright published by SPEED SPORT and TJSlideways. Austin McCarl finished second in the 410 standings, Justin Henderson third, JJ Hickle fourth and Brian Brown fifth.

Knoxville Raceway announced at the banquet that the 2027 Knoxville Championship Series point fund for the Leighton State Bank 410s will increase by $48,000, according to the report. The 2027 champion will receive $40,000, up from $20,000 in 2026, which KNIA-KRLS also reported. The 2027 fund pays $20,000 for second, $15,000 for third, $12,000 for fourth, $10,000 for fifth and $2,000 each for 15th through 20th. The report said more than $300,000 in cash and contingencies was handed out at the banquet, and the Knoxville Raceway Charitable Organization announced a $2,000 benefit for each of the 58 drivers who were point eligible in 2026.

Tasker Phillips was honored as the 360 champion for the second time, according to the report, with Kade Higday second, Clint Garner third, Cam Martin fourth and Sawyer Phillips fifth. JJ Hickle won the 360 owner points. J Kinder won the Pro Sprint title, his third career championship, ahead of Tyler Thompson, Chase Young, Mike Mayberry and Casey Friedrichsen.

Rookie of the Year honors went to Rees Moran in the 410 class, Evan Semerad in the 360 class and Matthew Botts in the Pro Sprints, according to SPEED SPORT, TJSlideways and KNIA-KRLS. Timms also received the 410 Lyle Boyd Hard Charger Award and the 410 feature lap leader award, and Brian Brown received the 410 Hoosier Tire Quick Time Award. Longtime race director John McCoy, who is retiring from that role, received the Ralph Capitani Heritage Award from National Sprint Car Hall of Fame & Museum executive director Bob Baker.

Sources: SPEED SPORT (Bill Wright, Nov. 22, 2026); TJSlideways (Bill Wright); KNIA-KRLS (Derek Cardwell, Nov. 22, 2026; rookies, point fund increase, awards); Knoxville Raceway (2026 point fund).
