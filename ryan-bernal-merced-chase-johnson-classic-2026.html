Ryan Bernal Wins Non-Wing Feature at Merced Chase Johnson Classic

Bernal won the Ultimate Sprint Car Series feature at Merced Speedway on Saturday, Nov. 21. Jadon Rogers was second and Ricky Lewis third.

Published November 22, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Ryan Bernal won the non-winged Ultimate Sprint Car Series feature at Merced Speedway’s Chase Johnson Classic in Merced, California, on Saturday, Nov. 21, according to results posted by Merced Speedway. The track listed Bernal first in the No. 73, ahead of Jadon Rogers, Ricky Lewis, Jacob Tuttle and Ryan Timmons.

SPEED SPORT also reported that Bernal won the non-winged feature on the same program. TJSlideways published a finishing order that matches Merced Speedway’s posted top five. The official MyRacePass event listing for the Chase Johnson Classic listed 21 entries in the Ultimate Sprint Car Series non-winged class.

The Saturday card at Merced was a tripleheader. Buddy Kofoid won the USAC National Midget feature, according to USAC, and Daison Pursley won the winged 360 feature.

Sources: Merced Speedway posted results (top five); Merced Speedway / MyRacePass event listing (Ultimate Sprint Car Series class, 21 entries); SPEED SPORT (Nov. 22, 2026, Bernal win); TJSlideways (finishing order); USAC (midget feature).
