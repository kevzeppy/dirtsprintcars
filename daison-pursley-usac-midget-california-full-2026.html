Daison Pursley to Run All Seven USAC Midget Races in California

The 2024 series champion will drive the CB Industries No. 86 throughout the Golden State Invasion, including the remaining events after the Hangtown cancellation.

Published November 17, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Daison Pursley will compete in all seven USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midget races on the California swing this month. The 2024 champion will drive the CB Industries No. 86.

The schedule originally included Placerville (canceled), Plaza Park (postponed), Merced Speedway (Nov. 20-21), Bakersfield (Nov. 24), and Ventura Raceway for the Turkey Night Grand Prix (Nov. 28). Officials are working to reschedule the postponed Plaza Park date within the invasion window.

Pursley’s full schedule commitment adds a title contender to the remaining California events as the series wraps its 2026 campaign.

Sources: USAC Racing on X; USAC schedule updates.
