Kyle Cummins Wins Second Straight USAC National Sprint Car Title

Cummins clinched the 2026 title Oct. 22 at Central Arizona Raceway with two races left. He won a career-high 11 features and finished 183 points ahead of Justin Grant.

Published November 12, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Kyle Cummins of Princeton, Indiana, won the 2026 USAC AMSOIL National Sprint Car championship, his second straight series title. He clinched it Oct. 22 with a third-place finish in the Western World Championships opener at Central Arizona Raceway in Casa Grande, Arizona, with two races left, USAC reported. The result also gave Petty Performance Racing its second straight entrant title with the No. 3p.

Cummins finished with 3,333 points, 183 ahead of Justin Grant (3,150), according to USAC’s final standings. Mitchel Moles was third with 3,075, Jake Swanson fourth with 2,873 and Kevin Thomas Jr. fifth with 2,657. Cummins led the series with a career-high 11 wins and 351 laps led in 48 starts, and he tied Grant for the most top-10 finishes with 45.

Cummins took the points lead in the eighth race of the season, in early April, and kept it the rest of the year. He set a USAC National Sprint Car record with 45 consecutive top-10 finishes, breaking the mark of 29 set by Thomas in 2025. His wins included his first USAC victories at the Terre Haute Action Track half-mile, in April’s Jim Hurtubise Classic; at Eldora Speedway, in May; and at Knoxville Raceway, in May’s Corn Belt Clash. He returned to Eldora in September to win the 4-Crown Nationals, and he also won the Indiana Sprint Week and Eastern Storm titles.

“I think I’m more excited about this one than I was the first one,” Cummins told USAC. “I was always saying that just one would be cool. You can have a lucky year and it felt like everything went our way last year. To come out this year and back it up, I don’t know if everything went our way, but we definitely had a really good season.”

Shortly after the final race, Petty Performance Racing announced its national team would shut down and not continue into 2027, USAC reported. Cummins is pursuing ownership and partnership options to keep the team together for 2027, DirtSprintCars.com reported Nov. 6.
