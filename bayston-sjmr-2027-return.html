Spencer Bayston Returning to Stenhouse Jr./Marshall Racing for 2027 World of Outlaws Season

Bayston confirmed during the World Finals that he will drive the NOS Energy Drink No. 17 again in 2027. He and crew chief Kyle Pruitt are looking to improve after what he called “a rebuilding year.”

Published November 9, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Spencer Bayston will return to Stenhouse Jr./Marshall Racing’s NOS Energy Drink No. 17 for the 2027 World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series season. Bayston confirmed the plan to Jacob Seelman of SWN on Friday, Nov. 6, during the World Finals at The Dirt Track at Charlotte. According to Seelman, Bayston said he and crew chief Kyle Pruitt have a lot they want to improve on to get the team past what he called “a rebuilding year.”

The 2026 season was Bayston’s first with the team owned by Ricky Stenhouse Jr. and Richard and Jennifer Marshall, which hired him to replace Sheldon Haudenschild with Pruitt, formerly of Shark Racing, as crew chief. The pairing won the season opener on Feb. 4 at Volusia Speedway Park, where Bayston started 10th and passed Anthony Macri for the lead on lap 16. World of Outlaws statistics credit Bayston with one win, 15 top-five finishes and 36 top-10s in 68 feature starts in 2026. He finished 10th, 22nd and 12th in the three World Finals features.

Bayston, of Lebanon, Ind., was the 2022 Kevin Gobrecht Rookie of the Year with CJB Motorsports and has six career World of Outlaws wins. Sunday night at the World of Outlaws Awards Celebration in Charlotte, SJMR crew member Ace Pruitt, Kyle Pruitt’s son, was named the series’ Jason Johnson Sportsman of the Year.

The 2027 World of Outlaws season begins Feb. 10-13 at Volusia Speedway Park’s Federated Auto Parts DIRTcar Nationals.
