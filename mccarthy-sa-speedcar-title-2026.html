Nov 4: Jack McCarthy Brings SA Speedcar Title Back Home

After 16 long seasons, the South Australian Speedcar crown is finally home. McCarthy held the high line like he was glued to the fence at Murray Bridge.

Published November 4, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Jack McCarthy has delivered a landmark victory for South Australian speedcar racing, claiming the 76th SA Speedcar Championship at Murray Bridge Speedway. He took the chequered flag ahead of reigning Australian champion Kaidon Brown and Victorian Caleb Mills, bringing the title back to SA for the first time since Ben Dillon’s victory in 2010-11.

Starting from third after qualifying heats, McCarthy capitalized when Brown and Mitch Saunderson made contact on the opening lap, sending Brown to the rear. McCarthy took the lead and held the high line throughout the 30-lap feature despite Brown’s strong charge back into second. Multiple cautions brought the pair together on restarts, but McCarthy stayed composed on the fence.

McCarthy called it “probably the best 30 laps I’ve ever done in a race car in my life.” The win adds to a solid weekend that also saw Terry Rankin claim the King of the Bridge title in the formula 500s.

Speedcars (non-winged) remain a staple of Australian dirt racing, and McCarthy’s result is a big one for the home-state fans.
