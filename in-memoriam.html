In Memoriam

Remembering drivers who lost their lives racing sprint cars and midgets. Listed by calendar day, newest first; each entry is confirmed by at least two independent sources.

September

Sep 20
2018410 Sprint Car (Central Pennsylvania)

Greg Hodnett

BAPS Motor Speedway, York Haven, PA

The veteran central Pennsylvania 410 sprint car driver was fatally injured in a crash at BAPS Motor Speedway; reports cite a steering failure that sent his car into the pit-entrance wall.

Wikipedia: no standalone article; covered in List of driver deaths in motorsport (matches date/track).

Sources: apnews.com pennlive.com Wikipedia

August

Aug 9
2014Empire Super Sprints

Kevin Ward Jr.

Canandaigua Motorsports Park, Canandaigua, NY

After a lap-14 incident with Tony Stewart sent him into the wall, the 20-year-old left his car under caution and was struck by Stewart's car. He was pronounced dead at the hospital. A grand jury declined to bring charges.

Wikipedia: covered in Tony Stewart article and driver-deaths list (matches).

Sources: nascar.com cnn.com Wikipedia
Aug 7
2016USAC National Midget (Belleville Midget Nationals)

Bryan Clauson

Belleville High Banks, Belleville, KS

While leading the Belleville Midget Nationals on Aug. 6, Clauson made contact with a lapped car, flipped and was struck by another car. He died the following evening at Bryan Medical Center West in Lincoln, Neb., aged 27. An organ donor, his legacy lives on through the BC39 and Driven2SaveLives.

Crash Aug 6; died Aug 7.

Sources: trans.usacracing.com indystar.com Wikipedia
Aug 4
2013410 Sprint Car (Central Pennsylvania)

Kramer Williamson

Lincoln Speedway, Abbottstown, PA

The 63-year-old veteran in the familiar pink No. 73 crashed during a qualifying race, striking the Turn 2 wall and flipping. He died the next day at York Hospital.

Crash Aug 3, 2013 (confirmed by Wikipedia); died Aug 4.

Sources: espn.com usatoday.com Wikipedia

July

Jul 21
1990USAC National Sprint Car (Joe James/Pat O'Connor Memorial)

Rich Vogler

Salem Speedway, Salem, IN

Leading near the white flag, Vogler crashed off Turn 4 and suffered fatal head injuries, five days before his 40th birthday. The race reverted to the last completed lap and he was declared the winner — his final USAC feature win.

Win total differs by source (Wikipedia: 170th win) so it's left out.

Sources: usacracing.com latimes.com Wikipedia

June

Jun 24
2018World of Outlaws Sprint Car Series (Jim Boyd Memorial)

Jason Johnson

Beaver Dam Raceway, Beaver Dam, WI

Battling for the lead on Lap 18 on June 23, Johnson crashed on the backstretch and was airlifted to Aurora Medical Center, where he died of his injuries the next day, aged 41.

Crash June 23, 2018; died June 24. Wikipedia lists the event date (June 23) only.

Sources: worldofoutlaws.com apnews.com Wikipedia
Jun 12
2013410 Sprint Car (three-time USAC National Midget champion)

Jason Leffler

Bridgeport Speedway, Swedesboro/Logan Twp., NJ

Running second in a heat race, Leffler's car suffered a front-suspension failure that locked the steering; it struck the wall and flipped. He died of a blunt-force neck injury, aged 37.

Sources: nascar.com inquirer.com Wikipedia

March

Mar 25
2017Southern Sprint Car Shootout Series (two-time USAC Silver Crown champion)

Dave Steele

Desoto Speedway, Bradenton, FL

On the opening lap of the feature, Steele's left-front wheel made contact with another car's right rear, sending his car airborne into the Turn 1 wall. He was pronounced dead at the scene, aged 42.

Wikipedia: no standalone article found; list entry matches date/track/description.

Sources: history.usacracing.com espn.com Wikipedia
