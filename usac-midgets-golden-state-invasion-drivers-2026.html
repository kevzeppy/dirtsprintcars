USAC National Midgets Golden State Invasion: Pursley, Day Confirmed for Full Schedule

The 2024 series champion Daison Pursley will run all seven California races in the CB Industries No. 86. Corey Day returns in Willie Kahne's No. FK4. Jett Barnes to make series debut.

Published November 10, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midgets open the Golden State Invasion this week with the Hangtown 100 at Placerville Speedway on Nov. 13-14. The seven-race California swing concludes with the Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura Raceway on Nov. 28.

Daison Pursley, the 2024 USAC National Midget champion, is entered for all seven events in the CB Industries No. 86. Corey Day will also contest the full schedule in Willie Kahne's No. FK4. Day has wins at Merced and is the two-time defending Turkey Night Grand Prix champion at Ventura.

Jett Barnes, the 2026 Chili Bowl Rookie of the Year, will make his USAC National Midget debut during the invasion, driving the CB Industries No. 83 at Plaza Park and Ventura.

Other confirmed stops: Plaza Park Raceway (Nov. 18), Merced Speedway Chase Johnson Classic (Nov. 20-21), Bakersfield Speedway Jason Leffler Memorial (Nov. 24), and Ventura (Nov. 28).

Sources: USAC Racing (@USACNation) posts on X, Nov. 4, 9 and 10, 2026; FloRacing 2026 USAC National Midgets schedule.
