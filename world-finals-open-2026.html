Nov 4: World of Outlaws World Finals Open at Charlotte

All dirt roads lead to Concord as David Gravel looks to join the exclusive three-peat club at the Dave Smith Motors World of Outlaws World Finals.

Published November 4, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The Greatest Show on Dirt wraps its 49th season this week at The Dirt Track at Charlotte. Four nights of action (Nov. 4-7) will crown the 2026 World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car champion, with Late Models and Super DIRTcar Series Modifieds along for the ride.

David Gravel and Big Game Motorsports hold a comfortable 124-point lead over Michael Kofoid. Only Steve Kinser, Donny Schatz and Brad Sweet have ever won three straight titles. Gravel can become the fourth if he keeps the No. 2 out of trouble. The math is simple: finish 20th or better in each of the three features and the trophy is his. Anything worse and the calculators come out.

Stout rosters are expected, including the Pennsylvania Posse and a healthy contingent of High Limit regulars looking for one last payday. Qualifying opens the week, with full programs Thursday through Saturday. Watch it all on DIRTVision if you can't make the trip.

The dirt is ready. The lights are on. Time to see who leaves Charlotte as champion.
