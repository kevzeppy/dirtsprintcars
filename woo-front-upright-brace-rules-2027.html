Nov 24: World of Outlaws posts 2027 front upright brace rules bulletin

After discussions led by the National Sprint Car Council, chassis manufacturers recommended a minimum 1.250-inch diameter by 0.065-inch wall front upright brace. All chassis must meet the specification after January 1, 2027.

Published November 24, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series officials posted a 2027 rules bulletin on the front upright brace on November 24, 2026. The bulletin states that after several discussions led by the National Sprint Car Council, sprint chassis manufacturers recommended the minimum dimension of the front upright brace to be 1.250 inches diameter by 0.065-inch wall thickness round tubing, ASTM 4130 normalized steel or equivalent material.

After January 1, 2027, all chassis will be required to meet this specification as written in the World of Outlaws Sprint Car Series Rule Book, Chapter 3, Section 5, V., d. The front cockpit upright brace must be installed and attached to the left and right front uprights and hip rails. The position of the brace on the upright and hip rail will be up to the expertise of the original chassis manufacturer.

The bulletin references an updated drawing 16.5 (updated 11/12/26). This follows earlier 2026 rule updates that had recommended extended front upright support bars, with the expectation they would become mandatory in 2027 after further study.

Sources: Rules Bulletin: Chassis, Front Upright Brace – 11-24-26, DIRTcar Rules (November 24, 2026); World of Outlaws social media announcement.
