Stevie Sussex Wins USAC Southwest Sprint Car Feature at Central Arizona Raceway (Nov. 14, 2026)

Stevie Sussex raced to his fourth USAC Avanti Windows & Doors Southwest Sprint Car victory of the year on Saturday night at Central Arizona Raceway in Casa Grande, Arizona.

Starting seventh in the 25-lap main event, the Tempe native took the lead from Charles Davis Jr. on lap 17 and led the final nine laps. It was his 17th career USAC Southwest feature win, third-most all-time.

Charles Davis Jr. finished second, R.J. Johnson third, Cameron La Rose fourth, and Dayton Shelton fifth.
