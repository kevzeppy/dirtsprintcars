Chelby Hinton Wins USAC East Coast Non-Winners Showdown at Bridgeport

Hinton took the lead on lap 6 for his first sprint car win.

Published October 31, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Chelby Hinton won the 20-lap Malley Pool Water Non-Winners Showdown for the USAC East Coast Sprint Cars presented by Baer Den Farms on Friday, Oct. 30, at Bridgeport Motorsports Park in Swedesboro, N.J. Hinton started 3rd in the Hogue Racing Enterprises No. 39 and took the lead on lap 6.

The non-points event was open to drivers who had never won a USAC East Coast feature. It was Hinton’s first sprint car win. He was recently crowned USAC Silver Crown Max Papis Innovations Rookie of the Year.

Top 5: 1. Chelby Hinton, 2. Kyle Spence, 3. Joe Kata, 4. Jonathan Swanson, 5. Ronald Helmick.

The series closes its season Saturday, Oct. 31, with the Baer Den Farms finale at Bridgeport, paying $2,500 to win.
