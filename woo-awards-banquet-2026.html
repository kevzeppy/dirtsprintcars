Gravel Honored for Third Straight Title, Kyle Ripper Earns Two Awards at World of Outlaws Banquet

Kyle Ripper was named Crew Chief of the Year and received the NOS Energy Drink Eloy Gutierrez Spirit Award. Sheldon Haudenschild was voted Most Popular Driver, and Emerson Axsom was named Rookie of the Year.

Published November 9, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Kyle Ripper received two honors Sunday night, Nov. 8, at the 2026 World of Outlaws Awards Celebration in Charlotte, N.C., the night after the World Finals closed the World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series season. Ripper, crew chief for Sheldon Haudenschild on the KCP Racing entry, was named the series’ Crew Chief of the Year and also received the NOS Energy Drink Eloy Gutierrez Spirit Award, according to the World of Outlaws. The series described Ripper as Haudenschild’s longtime crew chief and said he “continues to be entirely dedicated to Sprint Car Racing and the World of Outlaws.”

Haudenschild was named the 2026 World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Most Popular Driver, an award decided by fan voting, according to the series. Emerson Axsom received the Kevin Gobrecht Rookie of the Year award presented by Five Star Bodies. Axsom opened 2026 by winning the Chili Bowl Nationals and earned his first World of Outlaws feature win Sept. 6 at Huset’s Speedway in the Klaasmeyer/Petry Motorsports No. 27. Series statistics credit him with one win, 11 top-five finishes and 32 top-10s in 70 feature starts this season.

Announcer Johnny Gibson received the Ted Johnson Award after his 30th season calling World of Outlaws Sprint Car races. “I’m the luckiest Sprint Car fan in the world,” Gibson said in a video posted by the series. Ace Pruitt of Stenhouse Jr./Marshall Racing was named the Jason Johnson Sportsman of the Year. Brian Morrison was again named Engine Builder of the Year; the series noted that Morrison Racing Engines powers Big Game Motorsports and KCP Racing, the teams that finished first and third in the 2026 standings.

The banquet followed David Gravel’s official crowning Saturday night at The Dirt Track at Charlotte. Gravel won his third consecutive World of Outlaws championship with Big Game Motorsports, finishing the season with 11 wins, 48 top-five finishes and 66 top-10s in 75 starts, according to the series. He joins Steve Kinser, Donny Schatz and Brad Sweet as the only drivers to win at least three straight series titles. The 2027 season opens Feb. 10-13 at Volusia Speedway Park.
