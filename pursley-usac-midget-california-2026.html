Daison Pursley Is the Defending Hangtown 100 Winner as USAC Midgets Return to California

The 2024 USAC National Midget champion won last year’s Hangtown 100 with CB Industries. The series opens its seven-race California swing at Placerville on Nov. 13-14.

Published November 9, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Daison Pursley of Locust Grove, Oklahoma, won the most recent Hangtown 100 on Nov. 15, 2025, at Placerville Speedway, finishing 0.257 seconds ahead of Steven Snyder Jr. in the CB Industries No. 86, according to USAC. He is the 2024 USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midget champion.

The USAC National Midgets open their seven-race Golden State Invasion with the Hangtown 100 on Nov. 13-14, 2026, USAC said.

Sources: USAC, 2025 Hangtown 100 recap (Richie Murray, Nov. 15, 2025); USAC 2026 National Midget schedule (Richie Murray, Dec. 10, 2025).
