Tyler Nelson Repeats as USAC Midwest Thunder SpeeD2 Midget Champion

The Olathe, Kansas driver won three features and finished 40 points ahead of Bryce Massingill in the 13-race series, becoming the third driver to win consecutive Midwest Thunder titles.

Published November 10, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Tyler Nelson of Olathe, Kansas successfully defended his USAC Midwest Thunder SpeeD2 Midget championship in 2026. The 29-year-old won three features in the 13-race series, added a series-leading 10 top-five finishes, 13 top-10s, three fast qualifying times and seven heat-race wins, and beat Bryce Massingill of Troy, Ohio by 40 points in the final standings, 941-901.

Nelson had finished fourth at Moler Raceway Park, third at Lawrenceburg Speedway and second at Lincoln Park Speedway in 2025. This year he won at all three tracks and quadrupled his career win total from one to four. In May at Williamsburg, Ohio’s Moler Raceway Park, he led all 25 laps in wire-to-wire fashion and won by 6.054 seconds. In August at Lawrenceburg, in a result he called “the biggest win of my career,” he charged from seventh to first in the first two laps. In September at Putnamville, Indiana’s Lincoln Park Speedway, he went from fifth to first in the next-to-last race and took the point lead for good.

Nelson locked up the title with a fifth-place finish in the October finale at Montpelier Speedway. He became the third driver in the 11-year history of the series to win consecutive championships, following Aaron Leffel (2017, 2018 and 2019) and Jacob Denney (2020 and 2021). The point lead had changed hands among Nelson, Matt Lux and Massingill for much of the season.

Massingill finished in the top three of the standings for the sixth year in a row and was the runner-up in 2026. The series’ all-time winningest driver, with 23 victories, added five more wins in the latter half of the year — two at Moler, plus Millstream, Circle City and Montpelier — after a 364-night winless stretch, and led a series-high 96 laps. Lux finished third. Kole Kirkman was named Rookie of the Year after finishing sixth. Six drivers started all 13 points-paying features: Stratton Briggs, Buddy Hollmeyer, Massingill, Nelson, Parker Perry and Brayden Schwartz.
