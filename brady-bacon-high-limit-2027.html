Brady Bacon goes full-time High Limit with Vermeer in 2027

The four-time USAC champ brings the bacon (and the 55) to the High Limit tour full-time next year. No more part-time raids.

Published October 22, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Brady Bacon will run the full Interstate Batteries High Limit Racing schedule in 2027 with Vermeer Motorsports. The Indiana native, a four-time USAC National Sprint Car champion, joins the team after a partial 2026 campaign that produced four top-fives in 13 starts.

Tony Vermeer named Bacon the driver after a lengthy meeting, aiming for consistency and championship contention. Bacon called it an opportunity to build on the chemistry already shown with crew chief Tye Wolfgang.
