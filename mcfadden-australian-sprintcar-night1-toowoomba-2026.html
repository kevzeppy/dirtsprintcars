James McFadden Wins Australian Sprintcar Championship Night 1 at Toowoomba

McFadden charged to the Preliminary A Main win on the opening night of the Nutrend Homes Australian Sprintcar Championship, holding off Ryan Newton by 1.03 seconds.

Published November 20, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

James McFadden (WX23) stormed through to victory in the Preliminary A Main on Night 1 of the 64th Nutrend Homes Australian Sprintcar Championship at Hi-Tec Oils Toowoomba Speedway on Friday, November 20, 2026. The Western Australian driver started his chase for a fourth Australian title with a win over Ryan Newton (Q66) by 1.03 seconds, with Brock Hallett (Q5) finishing third.

Lachlan McHugh (NQ7), who had led much of the race and held the points lead entering the feature, fell to fourth in the closing stages after a late charge. Liam Williams (NQ26) rounded out the top five. McFadden took the lead with six laps remaining on a dive to the inside of Turn 4.

The 20-lap feature featured cautions, including an early incident involving Marcus Dumesny and Henry Titman, and a later spin by Randy Morgan. McHugh still led the overall points after Night 1 by a narrow margin over Hallett, with McFadden moving into third.

The championship continues Saturday night at Toowoomba.

Source: Speedcafe.com (Nov. 20, 2026). Additional reporting from Sprint Car Ratings and event coverage.
