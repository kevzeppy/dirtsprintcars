USAC Night of Champions Set for Dec. 11 in Indianapolis

The 71st annual banquet at the Indiana Roof Ballroom will honor USAC’s 2026 titlists and special award winners. Tickets are $75, and the RSVP deadline is Dec. 4.

Published November 13, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

The United States Auto Club will hold its 71st annual Night of Champions on Friday, Dec. 11, at the Indiana Roof Ballroom in Indianapolis, USAC announced. The banquet will honor USAC’s 2026 champions and special award winners. A cocktail hour starts at 6 p.m. Eastern, with opening ceremonies at 6:30 p.m., followed by dinner and the awards, according to USAC Media’s Richie Murray.

Tickets are $75 each and include the reception, dinner and two drinks, USAC said. Children’s tickets are $40. The RSVP deadline is Friday, Dec. 4, and USAC said prices will go up after that date. Tickets are sold through USAC’s online ticket page and will be available for pickup at the ballroom on the night of the banquet.

The NASCAR Youth Series Quarter Midget awards banquet will be held earlier the same day, at 3 p.m. Eastern at the Indiana Roof Ballroom, with light food and drinks from 2 p.m., according to USAC. That event is free, but a quarter midget banquet ticket does not admit the holder to the Night of Champions. Each NASCAR Youth Series champion will receive one champion banner, and extra banners cost $25, USAC said.

USAC has already named several 2026 champions in its season reviews. Kyle Cummins won the AMSOIL National Sprint Car title, Steven Drevicki the East Coast Sprint Car title, Jack Wagner the MRA Sprint Car title, R.J. Johnson the CRA Sprint Car title, Tyler Nelson the Midwest Thunder SpeeD2 Midget title and Broedy Graham the Western States Midget title. The NOS Energy Drink National Midget title is still open. Five races remain, starting Nov. 18 at Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia, California, and Jacob Denney leads Kevin Thomas Jr. by 73 points, according to USAC.
