Tanner Thorson Wins Trophy Cup Night 2 at Thunderbowl

Thorson took the win on the final lap after leader Kaleb Montgomery lost power.

Published October 31, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Tanner Thorson won the 30-lap 410 winged sprint car feature on Night 2 of Trophy Cup 32 on Friday, Oct. 30, at Thunderbowl Raceway in Tulare, Calif. Thorson started 4th in Kevin Kozlowski’s Works Limited car and earned $7,500.

Polesitter Kaleb Montgomery led the race until he lost power on the final lap, and Thorson went by for the win.

Top 10: 1. Tanner Thorson, 2. Daison Pursley, 3. D.J. Netto, 4. Cole Macedo, 5. Landon Brooks, 6. Justin Peck, 7. Tanner Holmes, 8. Carson Macedo, 9. Max Mittry, 10. Justin Sanders.

Night 3, the Trophy Cup finale, is Saturday, Oct. 31. TJSlideways reports championship night was moved up to the afternoon because of weather.
