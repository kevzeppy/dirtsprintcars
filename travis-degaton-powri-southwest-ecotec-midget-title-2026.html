Travis DeGaton Wins 2026 POWRi Southwest Ecotec Midget Championship

DeGaton won a series-leading seven features and took the title by 80 points over Shon Deskins, POWRi announced Nov. 17.

Published November 23, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Travis DeGaton of Sacramento, California, won the 2026 POWRi Avanti Windows & Doors Southwest Ecotec Midget Series championship, POWRi announced Nov. 17. DeGaton won a series-leading seven features and finished 80 points ahead of Shon Deskins of Waddell, Arizona.

Driving the No. 14X, DeGaton recorded 13 top-five finishes and 14 top-10 finishes in 17 feature starts, according to POWRi. His seven victories brought his career total in the series to 10, the most in series history. POWRi said he earned his first win of the season on May 9 at Adobe Mountain Speedway, won again on May 24 during Salute to Indy weekend after setting quick time and earning High Point Qualifier honors, started on the pole and led 16 of 20 laps at Deuce of Clubs Thunder Raceway in August for his sixth win, and closed the season with his seventh victory at Adobe Mountain.

DeGaton finished with 4,730 points. Deskins was second with 4,650 points, one feature win, nine top-five finishes and 15 top-10 finishes in 17 starts. Grant Schaadt of San Tan Valley, Arizona, was third with 4,640 points, five feature wins and 15 top-five finishes in 16 starts, 10 points behind Deskins. POWRi said the top three drivers combined to win 13 of the season’s 18 features and finished within 90 points of one another. Gary DeWitt of Mesa, Arizona, was fourth with 4,080 points, one feature win, six top-fives and 14 top-10s. Tyler Ruth of Glendale, Arizona, was fifth with 4,030 points, seven top-fives and 13 top-10s in 16 starts.

Sources: Travis DeGaton Captures 2026 POWRi Southwest Ecotec Midget Championship, POWRi (Nov. 17, 2026); DirtRacing.com (POWRi release, Nov. 17, 2026). Image: POWRi championship graphic.
