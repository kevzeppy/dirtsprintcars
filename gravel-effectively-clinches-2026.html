David Gravel Effectively Clinches Third Straight World of Outlaws Title

David Gravel and Big Game Motorsports leave Thursday at Charlotte 120 points ahead of Michael “Buddy” Kofoid. World of Outlaws said Gravel only needs to show up for the final two nights of the World Finals to make the title official.

Published November 6, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

David Gravel effectively clinched the 2026 World of Outlaws NOS Energy Drink Sprint Car Series championship with a sixth-place finish in Thursday’s 25-lap World Finals feature at The Dirt Track at Charlotte, according to the series’ official recap. World of Outlaws said Gravel and Big Game Motorsports “simply need to show up for the final two nights of racing to officially claim the title.” Rico Abreu won the feature; Kofoid, Gravel’s closest pursuer, finished fourth.

Official World of Outlaws standings updated Nov. 5 show Gravel at 9,870 points, 120 ahead of Kofoid (9,750). Sheldon Haudenschild is third, 258 back, after he was listed as a DNF on Thursday. Carson Macedo is fourth (-332) and Logan Schuchart fifth (-538). Gravel won the NOS Energy Drink first heat on Thursday before starting fifth in the feature, according to the official results.

The title would be Gravel’s third in a row with Big Game Motorsports. Through Thursday the series lists Gravel with 11 wins, 48 top fives and 65 top 10s in 73 starts. Kofoid has a series-high 17 wins in 72 starts with Roth Motorsports, according to the series standings. The World Finals continue Friday, Nov. 6 with a $15,000-to-win feature, and the season ends Saturday, Nov. 7 with a $25,000-to-win finale.

Sources: World of Outlaws (official Thursday recap, Nov. 5, 2026); World of Outlaws series points (updated Nov. 5, 2026); World of Outlaws feature results (Nov. 5, 2026).
