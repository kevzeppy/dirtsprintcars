Oct 23: Macedo wins wild Jason Johnson Classic in WoO Texarkana debut

Source: World of Outlaws

Carson Macedo and the Jason Johnson Racing No. 41 turned a chaotic 41-lapper into a storybook win on the World of Outlaws' first visit to Texarkana 67 Speedway. Seventh win of the year for Macedo, and the second time the JJR team has taken the Classic. David Gravel held on for second; Sheldon Haudenschild completed the podium.

If the racing looked messy from the stands, that's because it was. Dirt sprint cars and "clean" don't usually share a sentence, and Friday night was no exception. Macedo just happened to be the last one still pointed the right way when the checkers flew.
