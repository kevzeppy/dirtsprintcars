USAC National Midgets Make Plaza Park Debut Nov. 18

The USAC NOS Energy Drink National Midget Championship makes its first-ever appearance at Plaza Park Raceway in Visalia, California, on Wednesday, November 18, 2026.

The Good Earth Farms Special pays $5,000 to win over 40 laps on the 1/5-mile bullring. Front gates open at 5:30 p.m. PT with cars on track at 6 p.m. A-Class micros run as support. The series resumes after storms canceled the Hangtown 100 at Placerville.

Remaining California dates: Merced Speedway Nov. 20-21, Bakersfield (Kern Raceway) Nov. 24, and Turkey Night Grand Prix at Ventura Nov. 28. Watch live on FloRacing.

Sources: USAC Racing; Plaza Park Raceway; MyRacePass.
