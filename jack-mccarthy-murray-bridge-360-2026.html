Jack McCarthy Wins Murray Bridge 360/LS Track Championship Round 2

McCarthy started from the pole and led throughout Saturday at Murray Bridge Speedway in Murray Bridge, South Australia. The 360/LS feature was declared on lap 22 because of a curfew after Friday rain left the track greasy. Tate Cowie finished second and Hayden Vickers third.

Published November 18, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Jack McCarthy won Round 2 of the Lewiston Haulage Murray Bridge Speedway 360/LS Track Championship on Saturday, Nov. 14, at Murray Bridge Speedway in Murray Bridge, South Australia, according to a report posted on the track website. McCarthy, who had won the South Australian Speedcar Championship two weeks earlier, started the feature from the pole after a heat win and a runner-up finish and was not headed. Tate Cowie of Kadina finished second, and Hayden Vickers was third.

The track received a substantial amount of rain Friday, and the report said the surface was extremely greasy all evening, with turn two the most troublesome. The round drew 14 entries for three heats and the feature. McCarthy won heat 1 ahead of Hilton Rowett and Corey Sandow. Sandow, driving one of only two 360-cubic-inch powered cars in the field, then beat McCarthy and Brodie Somerfield in heat 2. Cowie won heat 3 over Vickers and Sam Martin. That put McCarthy and Sandow on the front row, followed by Cowie, Vickers, Somerfield, Rowett, Keke Falland, Martin, Round 1 winner Luke Squiers, Erin Virgin, Isla Rowett, Jack Will and Zack Sloan.

McCarthy led Sandow and Cowie early until a red flag on lap 4, when Sandow clashed with Will’s slower car. Sandow restarted at the rear and later retired. McCarthy then led Cowie and Vickers, with the three remaining close in lapped traffic, the report said.

The race ended on lap 22 when Martin spun in turn two and brought out a caution. With a curfew approaching, officials declared the finish. The track report said it was the second straight 360/LS sprint car feature at Murray Bridge that did not go the scheduled distance. Hilton Rowett finished fourth, followed by Somerfield, Squiers, Virgin, Sloan, Martin and Isla Rowett.

Source: McCarthy Is Now A Sprintcar Winner, Murray Bridge Speedway / Phil Michell Motorsport Media (Nov. 17, 2026).
