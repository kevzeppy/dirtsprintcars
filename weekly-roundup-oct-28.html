Weekly roundup

Week that was: Kofoid in Texarkana, Abreu’s High Limit crown, Miller’s Western World payday

A late restart in Arkansas, a rookie’s big check in Arizona, two champions crowned and an IndyCar driver headed for Turkey Night.

Published October 28, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

World of Outlaws: Kofoid wins the Short Track Nationals. Michael Kofoid waited for one more restart, then capitalized on it to win the Short Track Nationals at Texarkana 67 Speedway, his 17th win of the season, according to World of Outlaws. Carson Macedo’s big night turned into a so-close story.

World of Outlaws: Carson Macedo takes the Jason Johnson Classic. Friday’s Jason Johnson Classic went to Carson Macedo, per the World of Outlaws recaps. One Macedo got Friday’s trophy; the other led the most laps Saturday. Good weekend for the family, hard one for the trophy shelf.

High Limit: Abreu bags a second straight title. Rico Abreu only needed to keep Giovanni Scelzi in the mirrors at the Texas finale to win his second consecutive High Limit championship, as the Napa Valley Register reported. He remains the only champion the series has ever had.

USAC: Rookie Gavin Miller wins the Western World. Gavin Miller won the 59th Western World Championships at Central Arizona Raceway and collected $42,450, according to USAC. Some rookies get a parking spot; he got the check.

USAC: Swanson goes 2-for-2 in the preliminaries. Before Miller’s payday, Swanson swept both Western World preliminary nights, per USAC. Two nights in a row on that guitar trophy.

USAC: Cummins clinches the National Sprint Car title. Cummins locked up a second straight USAC National Sprint Car championship at Central Arizona with two races still left on the schedule, USAC reported. Homework done early.

Midgets: Christian Rasmussen to debut at Turkey Night. IndyCar driver Christian Rasmussen will trade the No. 21 Chevy for a dirt midget at the Turkey Night Grand Prix, USAC announced. Hope somebody tells him the gravy is on the track.

Australia: Manders wins Kwinana Powerpalooza. Manders survived a crash-filled feature, cut to 20 laps by the curfew, to win the Powerpalooza at Kwinana, as reported by Speedway Illustrated News. Even the clock in Perth wanted to go home early.

Coming up

  • World of Outlaws World Finals, The Dirt Track at Charlotte, Nov. 4–7
  • USAC Turkey Night Grand Prix, Nov. 27–28
  • High Limit International in Perth, from Dec. 28
Sources: World of Outlaws (Texarkana), World of Outlaws recaps, Napa Valley Register, USAC National Sprint news, USAC (Western World night two), USAC (Central Arizona), USAC (Rasmussen), Speedway Illustrated News. Results are as reported by those outlets.
