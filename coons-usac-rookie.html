Oct 29: Coons named USAC National Sprint Car Top Rookie for 2026

Source: USAC Racing

Cale Coons earned honors as the 2026 USAC AMSOIL Sprint Car National Championship Rookie of the Year. The son of USAC career Triple Crown champion Jerry Coons Jr. became the first son of a past National Sprint Car champ to take the rookie title with the series.

Genetics help, but you still have to turn the wheel and not crash the thing. Coons did both often enough to take home the hardware. Family business is good business when the trophy ends up in the same garage.
