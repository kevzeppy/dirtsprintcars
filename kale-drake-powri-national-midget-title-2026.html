Kale Drake Wins 2026 POWRi National Midget League Championship

Drake clinched his first national midget title for RMS Racing on the final weekend at I-44 Speedway and finished 310 points ahead of Karter Sarff.

Published November 12, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Kale Drake of Collinsville, Oklahoma, won the 2026 POWRi National Midget League championship driving for RMS Racing, POWRi announced Oct. 26. It is the first national midget title for both Drake and RMS Racing, according to Toyota. Drake, who won the POWRi Micro League title in 2023, is the first driver to win both the POWRi National Midget and Micro League championships, according to the league.

Drake clinched the title with a fourth-place finish in the Oct. 23 feature at I-44 Speedway in Oklahoma City, the first night of the season-ending weekend, Toyota Racing reported. Brecken Reese led all 30 laps to win that race. The championship became official after the Oct. 24 finale, in which Drake set quick time and started on the front row but was involved in a rollover on the opening lap and finished 22nd. Gaige Weldon won the finale.

In 21 starts, Drake had his first four POWRi National Midget feature wins, 16 top-five finishes, 18 top-10 finishes and five runner-up finishes, according to POWRi. He also led the league with eight heat race wins and added a stand-alone POWRi West Midget win.

Drake finished 310 points ahead of Karter Sarff of Mason City, Illinois. Kyle Jones of Kennedale, Texas, was third, 600 points back, followed by Reese, 820 back, and Drake Edwards, 1,310 back. The title qualifies Drake for the Race of Champions at the Chili Bowl Nationals. It is the third straight POWRi national midget title for a Toyota-powered driver, after Cannon McIntosh in 2024 and Jacob Denney in 2025, Toyota said.

Drake is also sixth in the USAC National Midget standings with 941 points heading into the series’ five-race California finish, which starts Nov. 18 at Plaza Park Raceway. Read the USAC National Midget schedule story.
