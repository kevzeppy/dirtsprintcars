Nov 4: Chase Dietz Jumps into Chase Briscoe’s #5 at World Finals

The nation’s winningest 410 sprint car driver this year is hopping into NASCAR star Chase Briscoe’s car for the World Finals. PA Posse meets stock-car royalty.

Published November 4, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Chase Dietz, the York, Pennsylvania driver who has racked up more 410 sprint car wins than anyone in the country in 2026, will pilot Chase Briscoe’s No. 5 at the Dave Smith Motors World of Outlaws World Finals at The Dirt Track at Charlotte, Nov. 4-7.

It’s Dietz’s first appearance at the track since 2015. Briscoe, who has been racing the car himself in select events, handed the seat over after announcing the deal. Dietz enters with a National Open win at Williams Grove already in the bag this season plus a Bricker’s Bash victory over the Outlaws on home turf.

The move adds another strong PA Posse presence to an already stout World Finals roster that includes Gravel, Kofoid, Schuchart, the Macedos and more. Whether Dietz can translate his local dominance into a World Finals win remains to be seen, but the car has been competitive when Briscoe has run it.
