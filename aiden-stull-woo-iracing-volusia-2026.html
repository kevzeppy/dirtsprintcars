Aiden Stull Wins World of Outlaws iRacing Pro Series Opener at Volusia

Stull led from early in the feature and held off Alex Bergeron to take the first win of the 2026 Pro Series Sprint Car Championship. He leads the points by one.

Published November 17, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Aiden Stull won the opening round of the 2026 World of Outlaws Pro Series Sprint Car Championship on iRacing at Volusia Speedway Park on Monday night, Nov. 16. The series announced the result on Nov. 17.

Stull took the lead early from Tanner Pettit and held off a charge from multi-time Volusia winner Alex Bergeron. Bergeron, who set quick time, spun after climbing to second and recovered for third. Stull now leads the championship by a single point over Bergeron, with Pettit third. The $25,000 purse for the five-race sprint car championship is on the line, with $6,000 to the champion.

The next round is scheduled for Huset’s Speedway on Nov. 23. Action is live on DIRTVision and iRacing social channels.
