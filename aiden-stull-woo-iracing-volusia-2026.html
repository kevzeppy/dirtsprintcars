Aiden Stull Wins Opening World of Outlaws iRacing Sprint Car Race at Volusia

November 17, 2026

Aiden Stull scored the first victory of the 2026 World of Outlaws iRacing Pro Series Sprint Car Championship at Volusia Speedway Park on Monday night, Nov. 17.

Thirty-five drivers qualified for the five-race series. Stull led the points by a single point over Alex Bergeron after the 35-lap feature. Tanner Pettit finished second. The championship pays $6,000 to the winner from a $25,000 total purse shared with the Late Model series.

The series continues at Huset’s Speedway on Nov. 23.
