Luke Dillon Wins King of Wings at Perth Motorplex; Tony Clasener Takes Latrobe Opener

Dillon started second and beat James Inglis and Andrew Priolo in Round 4 of the Maddington Toyota Sprintcar Series. In Tasmania, Clasener won after polesitter Tate Frost was listed as disqualified.

Published November 14, 2026 · DirtSprintCars.com

Luke Dillon won the Scardifields Smash Repairs King of Wings on Saturday, Nov. 14, at Perth Motorplex in Kwinana Beach, Western Australia, according to official results posted by the Maddington Toyota Sprintcar Series. Dillon, in the No. 44, started second in the 25-car field and won the 40-lap feature. Perth Motorplex billed the event as the 45th annual King of Wings, and it was Round 4 of the 2026-27 series, according to the series calendar.

James Inglis finished second from fourth, and Andrew Priolo was third after starting 12th, according to the series results. Jaydee Dack finished fourth and Brad Maiolo fifth from 14th. Jason Pryde, Daniel Harding, AJ Nash, Michael Keen and Jack Williamson completed the top 10. Polesitter Jason Kendrick finished 17th. Dayne Kingshott, who won the WA Sprintcar Title at Bunbury a week earlier, started third and did not finish, and Round 2 winner Callum Williamson also failed to finish, according to the results.

Kingshott posted the fastest time across the three qualifying groups, 13.229 seconds, according to the series results. Kendrick won the A Dash, Dillon won the B Dash and Callum Williamson won the Mid Pack Dash. Heat races went to Dack, Nash and Dillon. The series next races Dec. 5 at Perth Motorplex in the Jolly Good Auto Electrics John Day Classic, according to the Maddington Toyota Sprintcar Series schedule.

At Latrobe Speedway in Tasmania, Tony Clasener won the 25-lap sprint car A-main at the CJD Equipment Season Opener, according to official results posted on DirtEvents. Clasener started second in the eight-car field and finished 6.323 seconds ahead of Callum Roberts. Nick Penno was third, Kahley Luttrell fourth and Daniel Percy fifth. Denzil Sargent finished sixth, a lap down, and Chris Johns retired after six laps.

Tate Frost started on the pole and is listed as disqualified after completing 12 laps, according to the DirtEvents results, which do not give a reason. Frost won the first heat race, and Penno won the second. Latrobe Speedway had announced before the meeting that Frost, from Launceston, would race at the opener. The track said his career highlights include the opening-night feature win at Warrnambool’s 50th Grand Annual Sprintcar Classic in January 2023 and the 2024 SA/Vic Easter Sprintcar Trail.
